This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Ironwood Plastics, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Ironwood Plastics, an enclosure supplier, is based in Penang. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so enclosure suppliers like Ironwood Plastics supply the housings that protect the electronics of each device.

## Who to call

Isaac Sato is Larkspur's account contact at Ironwood Plastics. Start with him for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
