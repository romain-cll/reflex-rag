This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Redstone Plastics, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Redstone Plastics, an enclosure supplier, is based in Hsinchu. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so enclosure suppliers like Redstone Plastics supply the housings that protect the electronics of each device.

## Who to call

Julian Reyes is Larkspur's account contact at Redstone Plastics. Start with him for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
