Ridgeline Packaging is a packaging supplier on the procurement supplier list. They handle retail and shipping packaging: boxes, inserts, printed sleeves and the protective materials that get a device to a customer site in one piece.

## Location

Ridgeline Packaging is based in Suzhou.

## Contact

Simon Calloway is Larkspur's account contact at Ridgeline Packaging. Simon Calloway handles pricing requests, artwork and dieline questions, sample boxes, order status and escalations. Send requests to Simon rather than to other people at Ridgeline Packaging, so there is a single thread to follow.

## Working with them

- Packaging artwork and dielines live in the shared supplier folder; send links, not attachments.
- Procurement should be copied on anything involving price, schedule or terms.
- Log calls and meetings with Ridgeline Packaging in a meeting note that links back to this page.
