## Who they are

Cobalt Test Labs, a certification testing supplier, is based in Lake Forest. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so a testing lab such as Cobalt Test Labs is the kind of partner that checks a device against the certification requirements it has to meet.
