This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Keystone Sensing, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Keystone Sensing, a sensor module supplier, is based in Tijuana. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so sensor module suppliers such as Keystone Sensing provide the sensing parts at the heart of each device.

## Who to call

Kofi Weller is Larkspur's account contact at Keystone Sensing. Start with him for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
