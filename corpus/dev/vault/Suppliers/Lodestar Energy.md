Lodestar Energy is a battery pack supplier on the procurement supplier list. They assemble battery packs for electronic devices and provide the safety and shipping documentation that batteries require.

## Location

Lodestar Energy is based in Monterrey.

## Contact

Maya Ramsey is Larkspur's account contact at Lodestar Energy. Maya Ramsey is the person to reach for pricing, samples, safety and shipping documents, order status and escalations. Requests that need their engineers should go through Maya, with procurement copied, so nothing gets lost between teams.

## Working with them

- Procurement should be copied on anything involving price, schedule or terms.
- Log calls and meetings with Lodestar Energy in a meeting note that links back here.
