Cobalt Molding is an enclosure supplier on the procurement supplier list. As the name suggests, their core work is injection molding: plastic housings, covers and small structural parts for electronic products.

## Location

Cobalt Molding is based in Suzhou.

## Contact

Andre Abara is Larkspur's account contact at Cobalt Molding. Go to Andre Abara for pricing questions, tooling and sample discussions, order status and escalations. If engineering needs to talk to their tooling or quality people, ask Andre to set it up and keep procurement copied.

## Working with them

- Part drawings and revisions belong in the shared supplier folder, not in email attachments.
- Procurement should be in the loop on anything that touches price, schedule or terms.
- Record calls and meetings with Cobalt Molding in a meeting note linked back to this page.
