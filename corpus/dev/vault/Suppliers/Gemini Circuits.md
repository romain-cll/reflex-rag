This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Gemini Circuits, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Gemini Circuits, a circuit board supplier, is based in Dongguan. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so circuit board suppliers such as Gemini Circuits build the boards that carry the electronics of each device.

## Who to call

Yuki Pruitt is Larkspur's account contact at Gemini Circuits. Start with them for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
