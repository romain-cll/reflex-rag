This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Northstar Electronics, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Northstar Electronics, a circuit board supplier, is based in Suzhou. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so circuit board suppliers such as Northstar Electronics build the boards that carry the electronics of each device.

## Who to call

Amara Duarte is Larkspur's account contact at Northstar Electronics. Start with her for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
