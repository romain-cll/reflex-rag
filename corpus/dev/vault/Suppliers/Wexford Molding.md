This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Wexford Molding, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Wexford Molding, an enclosure supplier, is based in Monterrey. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so enclosure suppliers like Wexford Molding supply the housings that protect the electronics of each device.

## Who to call

Lucia Engel is Larkspur's account contact at Wexford Molding. Start with her for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
