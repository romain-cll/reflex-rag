This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Halcyon Compliance Labs, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Halcyon Compliance Labs, a certification testing supplier, is based in Boulder. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so a testing lab such as Halcyon Compliance Labs is the kind of partner that checks a device against the certification requirements it has to meet.

## Who to call

Mei Lund is Larkspur's account contact at Halcyon Compliance Labs. Start with her for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
