This page is kept by procurement as a quick reference on Tamarack Sensing, one of the suppliers Larkspur Devices, Inc. works with.

## Who they are

Tamarack Sensing, a sensor module supplier, is based in Ho Chi Minh City. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so sensor module suppliers such as Tamarack Sensing provide the sensing parts at the heart of each device.

Nadia Abara is Larkspur's account contact at Tamarack Sensing. Start with her for any question about the relationship, and let procurement know if you are in touch with the supplier so that nothing is handled twice.
