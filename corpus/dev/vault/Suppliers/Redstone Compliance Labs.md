Redstone Compliance Labs is a certification testing supplier on the procurement supplier list. As a certification lab, they run the compliance and safety testing a connected device needs before it can be sold, and they produce the test reports that go with it.

## Location

Redstone Compliance Labs is based in Boulder.

## Contact

Claire Petrov is Larkspur's account contact at Redstone Compliance Labs. Contact Claire Petrov for testing quotes, lab scheduling, questions about test plans or results, and escalations. Engineers who need to talk to a test engineer at the lab should ask Claire to set it up, so procurement can keep track of the scope.

## Working with them

- Testing scope and cost need procurement sign-off before anything is booked.
- Keep test plans, sample lists and reports in the shared supplier folder.
- Record calls and meetings with Redstone Compliance Labs in a meeting note that links back here.
