Still thinking about the design review. The question came up in [[2025-03-10 Atlas design review]], we talked about it for ages, and we walked out with nothing. Switching to USB-C power for Atlas is on the table, and the team still has not settled it.

I get that nobody wants to rush a change that touches the board and the enclosure. But every day we leave it open, I keep two versions of the power section in my head, and that is tiring. I would rather lose an argument than carry both designs around.

Going to write down what each option costs me on the electrical side and bring it to the next conversation. Maybe a page of facts will do what a long discussion did not.
