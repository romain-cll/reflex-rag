Still thinking about the news from the [[2025-04-23 All-hands]]: Hazel Osei is starting in a new role as Senior Product Manager on the Product team.

My first reaction was relief. On the hardware side we spend a lot of time guessing what matters most to customers, and a Senior Product Manager who owns those calls could make our priorities much clearer. Or it could mean more meetings. Probably both, if I'm honest.

I'd like to catch Hazel early and walk her through how we trade things off on the electrical side, so that the requests coming our way make sense for the boards we're actually building. My small hope: fewer last-minute surprises.

Anyway, I'm happy for her. It was a good all-hands overall, short and to the point, which I always appreciate.
