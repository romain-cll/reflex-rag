Weekend thoughts on Hector Haddad's new role. Hector Haddad works as Firmware Lead on the Firmware team, which was announced at the [[2025-11-05 All-hands]].

I'm happy about this one. Electrical and firmware work are tangled together on every board I touch, and a clear lead on the firmware side should make it easier to know who to ask when a pin does something unexpected. I also like that the role has a name attached, because it is less of a guessing game when a question comes up late in the day.

I told myself I would not start a new side project this month, so instead I will write down the questions I want to bring to Hector. Small steps.
