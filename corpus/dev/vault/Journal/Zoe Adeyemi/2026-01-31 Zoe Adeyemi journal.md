Stayed late to count the Fjord units on the bench, mostly because I wanted to see them all in one place. The Fjord EVT build produced 65 units. I went down the rows twice to be sure, and it was 65 units both times, each one with its little handwritten label.

It's a good feeling, seeing the kit as a real thing instead of a stack of drawings and a power budget. The housings look right, the boards are seated, and the bench is properly crowded now. Rosa keeps wandering over to poke at the mounts, and Hector has already claimed a corner for his firmware updates.

The full write-up is in the [[2026-01-29 Fjord EVT review]]. For me the job now is power measurements, and lots of them. Tired, but the happy kind of tired.
