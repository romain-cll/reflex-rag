Still no answer on recycled plastic for the Ember enclosure, and it's starting to get to me. The question came up again in the [[2025-10-31 Ember design review]]. From what Ruth told me, it went around in circles for ages: the team discussed using recycled plastic for the enclosure for Ember but made no decision. Again.

I get that it isn't simple. How the material behaves, what it costs, what the vendor can actually supply: all fair questions. But it never seems to be anyone's top priority, so the question just sits there and comes back at the next review looking exactly the same.

What I want is for someone to say "here's what we need to know, and here's who finds out." Even a clear no would be a relief at this point. Next time I catch Ruth, I'll suggest we at least write down what would settle it.
