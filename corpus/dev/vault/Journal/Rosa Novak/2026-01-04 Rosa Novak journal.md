Long week, and a good one. I helped on the Ember EVT build, so I was on the floor when the housings started coming together. I was not at the review itself, which is fine, but I did count the units myself at the end of the build, and the Ember EVT build produced 79 units. I counted them twice just to be sure, because it is the kind of number people quote from memory later.

[[2026-01-02 Ember EVT review]] was about this build, so I hope whoever wrote it up had the final count. Either way I feel good about how the enclosures went together. Watching a part you only knew from drawings turn into something you can hold never gets old.
