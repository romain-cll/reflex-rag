Still no answer on cellular backhaul for Granite, and it is starting to get to me. From what Tessa told me, the question took over most of the [[2026-03-24 Granite design review]], everyone made their case, and they walked out with nothing decided. Again.

I get that it's a real trade-off. But from where I sit it blocks everything. If there's a modem and an antenna in there, the inside of the enclosure changes, the vents might move, and half of what I've sketched goes in the bin. If there isn't, I can finally commit to a layout.

What bothers me most is that nobody seems to own the call. Everyone has an opinion and nobody has a deadline. I'm going to keep two layouts going in parallel for now, which is a waste, and ask Carmen whether someone can just decide.
