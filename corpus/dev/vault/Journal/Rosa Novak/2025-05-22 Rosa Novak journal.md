Still no answer on the e-ink display for Cirrus, and it's starting to get to me. The question came up in [[2025-05-20 Cirrus design review]], everyone had an opinion, and the team discussed adding an e-ink display for Cirrus at length but made no decision. So we're back where we started.

From where I sit it matters a lot. A display means a window in the front face, a different wall layout around it and probably new fixtures for testing. Without one, the front is a plain shell and the enclosure work is much simpler. I can't do much useful on the front of the housing until someone makes the call, so I keep doing side work and waiting.

I get that it's a real trade-off between battery, cost and what customers would like to see. I just wish we'd pick a direction, even a provisional one, instead of leaving it open and hoping it settles itself.
