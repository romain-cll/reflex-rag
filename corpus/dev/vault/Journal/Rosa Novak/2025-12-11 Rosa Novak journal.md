At the [[2025-12-08 All-hands]], Gabriel announced that Tomas Mbeki is starting in a new role as Senior Account Executive on the Sales team. I keep coming back to it, in a good way.

I don't cross paths with Sales much from the mechanical side. A Senior Account Executive who knows when to bring an engineer in early would make that a lot easier.

So I'm happy for Tomas. I'll tell him so next time I see him, and that he can send enclosure questions straight to me instead of waiting for them to bounce around.

Small win, but it put me in a good mood for the rest of the week.
