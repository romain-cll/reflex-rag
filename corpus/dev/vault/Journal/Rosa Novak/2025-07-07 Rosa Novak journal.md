Still annoyed about the design review. The question came up in [[2025-07-05 Dune design review]], we talked about it for ages, and we walked out with nothing. The team discussed offering the wall-mount bracket in white for Dune but made no decision.

I understand the arguments on the other side, I really do. A second color means more to manage on the build side. But the bracket is the one part people actually see on the wall, and every day we leave it open is a day I cannot lock the drawings.

I keep rehearsing the case for white in my head, which is not a good use of my time. Coffee first, then maybe it will stop bugging me.
