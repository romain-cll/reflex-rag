Heard in the hallway today that Fjord is going with Cobalt Molding for the enclosure. Nothing official has landed in my inbox, it was just a couple of people chatting by the coffee machine, but it lines up with what came out of the [[2025-12-05 Fjord enclosure review]], where we leaned that way mostly on price. So it sounds like the team chose to move forward with Cobalt Molding and that it is sticking.

Honestly, I'm relieved to have a direction. I've been sitting on the enclosure drawings waiting to know whose molding rules to design to. If it really is Cobalt Molding, I can start tightening the ribs and the vent pattern for their process and stop keeping two versions in my head.

Still, hallway talk is hallway talk. I'll wait for Diego or Ines to confirm before I send anything out.
