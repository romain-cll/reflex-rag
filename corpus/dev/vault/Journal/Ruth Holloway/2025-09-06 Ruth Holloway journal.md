Frustrated, and I want to write it down. The question came up in [[2025-09-04 Ember design review]] and we went around in circles. The team discussed using recycled plastic for the enclosure for Ember but made no decision, and as far as I can tell we still have not settled it.

I am not even against the idea. What bothers me is that the board layout depends on the enclosure, and every time the material stays open I have to keep two options alive in my head. Everyone agrees the question matters, everyone has an opinion, and nobody owns the answer. I would rather have a clear no than another meeting that ends with "let's keep it open". I will raise it again at the next sync and ask who is going to close it.
