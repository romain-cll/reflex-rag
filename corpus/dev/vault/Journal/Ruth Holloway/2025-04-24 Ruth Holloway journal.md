At the [[2025-04-23 All-hands]], Gabriel announced that Leah Ashby is starting in a new role as Account Executive on the Sales team. I've been thinking about it since.

Honestly, I'm pleased for her. From where I sit in Hardware Engineering, Sales can feel like a different planet, and most of what reaches us is a customer question that has been passed along a few times before it lands on my desk. An Account Executive who is willing to pull an engineer into a call early would save everyone a lot of back and forth.

I don't know Leah well yet, so this is me hoping more than knowing. I'll make a point of saying hi and offering help whenever one of her customers asks something about the sensors that needs an electrical answer.

Anyway, good news for a change, and it was nice to hear the room clap for someone.
