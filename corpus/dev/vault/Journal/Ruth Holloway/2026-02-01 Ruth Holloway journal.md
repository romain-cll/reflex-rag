I helped on the Fjord EVT build, but I was not in the room for the review, so all I have is what I saw on the bench. The [[2026-01-30 Fjord EVT review]] was about that build, and I hope my count gets to the people who were there. It was a long, satisfying stretch of work, and I am happy to see the first real units sitting on the shelf. Now I just want my desk back and a quiet afternoon.
