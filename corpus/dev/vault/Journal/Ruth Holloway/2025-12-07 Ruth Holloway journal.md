Spent most of the day with the EVT units on the bench. I lined them all up, labeled the ones that didn't have a tag yet, and counted: the Ember EVT build produced 65 units. I counted twice to be sure, because my head was still full of the numbers from the [[2025-12-05 Ember EVT review]] and I didn't trust myself.

It's a good feeling, honestly. A bench full of real boards in real enclosures after months of schematics and layout. The room smells like warm electronics and cardboard, which I've decided is my favorite smell.

Before anyone builds a spreadsheet on top of the wrong figure, I need to reconcile my count with the build records. Then bring-up starts for real.
