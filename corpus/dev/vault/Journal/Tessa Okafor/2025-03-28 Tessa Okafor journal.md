Still no answer on USB-C, and it's starting to get to me. I read the notes from the [[2025-03-26 Atlas design review]]: the team discussed switching to USB-C power for Atlas, went around in circles for most of the meeting, and made no decision. Everyone has homework, nobody has a date.

I get that it's a real trade-off. Batteries keep installs clean, a cable saves facilities a chore. But from where I sit it blocks a lot. I can't finish the internal layout without knowing whether there's a port, a seal and a cable path to plan around, or just a battery door. Every week this stays open is a week of work I might have to redo.

What I'd like is for someone to just pick, even provisionally, so the rest of us can move. Going to raise it with Hazel next week, politely. Maybe.
