I am frustrated, and I might as well write it down. The question came up in the design review, [[2025-05-14 Cirrus design review]], and we talked about it for ages: we discussed adding an e-ink display for Cirrus, and we still made no decision. Everyone had a fair point, and then we all went home with the question exactly where we found it.

For me it is not abstract. A display means an opening in the housing, and I cannot settle the enclosure drawings around a maybe. Every day it stays open is a day I design around both possible versions of the same part.

I am not asking for a particular answer. I just want an answer. Tomorrow I will ask Diego how we get this one closed, politely, with coffee.
