I am frustrated, and I might as well write it down. We discussed adding a cellular backhaul option for Granite in [[2025-12-24 Granite design review]], we talked about it at length, and we made no decision. Everyone had a good point, and that is exactly the problem: nobody wanted to be the one to close the question.

From the mechanical side I cannot plan around a maybe. While the question stays open, the enclosure work has to keep both possibilities in mind, and that slows everything down. I would rather get a clear yes or a clear no than another round of the same arguments. I will bring it up again at the next opportunity and push for a call.
