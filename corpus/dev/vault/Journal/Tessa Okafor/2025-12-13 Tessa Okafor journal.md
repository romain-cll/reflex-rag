Gabriel announced at the [[2025-12-08 All-hands]] that Hector Haddad is starting in a new role as Firmware Lead on the Firmware team. I've been mulling it over since.

As a mechanical engineer I don't write firmware, but I run into it all the time: a button that behaves differently than the drawing assumed, a status light that needs a different light pipe, a sensor that wants more airflow than the enclosure gives it. Having one Firmware Lead to go to should make those conversations faster, or at least shorter.

I'm glad for Hector. I'll ask him for a quick sync so the mechanical side knows how he wants to work with us now.

Feeling oddly optimistic about it, which is a nice change.
