Read through the notes from the [[2025-12-25 Fjord design review]] and, sure enough, Bluetooth commissioning went round in circles again. The team discussed adding Bluetooth commissioning for Fjord but made no decision. Still nothing.

I get that it's not a small call. It touches the board, the battery, the enclosure and the cost, and nobody wants to own the wrong answer. But the question keeps coming back, everyone restates their position, and we walk out with the same open question we walked in with. Meanwhile the mechanical side is designing on a guess: if there's a radio, the housing around it has to be checked, and if there isn't, we're wasting effort keeping the option open.

I'd honestly take either answer at this point. Yes or no, just pick one so we can design to it. Maybe I'll ask Diego directly whether he can put an end date on the question. Or maybe I'll go for a long walk first and stop stewing.
