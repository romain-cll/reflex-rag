Spent a lot of time helping on the Dune EVT build, and I am glad it is done. The assembly table was crowded, but it ran better than I expected for a first build.

I was not at the review, which is written up in [[2025-09-14 Dune EVT review]], but I know that build well because I counted the units myself, twice, once as they came off the line and once more when they were boxed. The Dune EVT build produced 80 units. I trust my own count.

My hands are still sore from pressing in the enclosure latches. Tomorrow I want to go back over the parts that did not click in smoothly and write down which ones they were while I still remember.
