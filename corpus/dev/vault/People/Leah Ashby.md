## Role

Leah Ashby works as Account Executive on the Sales team. She talks with owners and managers of commercial buildings about the connected air-quality sensors of Larkspur Devices, follows their questions through to an answer, and hands new customers over to the customer success group once they are on board.

## Office

Leah Ashby works out of the Chicago office. Her calendar is usually busy with customer calls, so the best way to reach her is a message on the team channel. She enjoys comparing notes with colleagues who have heard the same question from a customer, since it often shows what the product should say more clearly.
