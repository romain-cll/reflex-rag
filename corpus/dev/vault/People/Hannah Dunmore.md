Hannah Dunmore works as VP of Engineering on the Engineering team at Larkspur Devices, Inc. This page is a short profile so colleagues know who she is and where to find her.

## Role

As VP of Engineering, Hannah is the person engineers and other teams turn to for questions about how engineering work is organized at Larkspur. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources manufacturing, so a good part of that work happens alongside partners outside the company.

## Office

Hannah Dunmore works out of the Austin office. When you need her, check the shared calendar first and then send a message through the usual channels.
