Hannah Dunmore works as VP of Engineering on the Engineering team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As VP of Engineering, I am responsible for the hardware, firmware and software work behind our connected indoor air-quality sensors. I set engineering priorities with product, staff the teams and keep an eye on quality and schedules, so that what we promise customers is what we ship.

## Office

I am based in the Portland office.

## Working with me

I keep open office time for anyone in engineering who wants to talk through a problem, a career question or an idea. For decisions, I ask for a written proposal with the options considered, so the reasoning stays in the vault for whoever comes next.
