Clara Zielinski works as Head of Procurement on the Operations team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As Head of Procurement, I am responsible for how we buy: supplier selection, negotiations, contract terms and the sourcing approach behind our sensors. Since we outsource manufacturing, a large part of my job is keeping good relationships with our suppliers and contract manufacturers, and making sure engineering choices stay buildable at a sensible cost.

## Office

I work out of the Austin office.

## Working with me

Bring me in early when a design change touches a part we already buy, or when a supplier sends terms that look unusual. Routine orders and follow-ups can go straight to the procurement team, who handle the day-to-day work.
