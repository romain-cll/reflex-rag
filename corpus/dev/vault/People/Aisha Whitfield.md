Aisha Whitfield works as Account Executive on the Sales team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As an Account Executive, Aisha works with commercial building owners and operators who are looking at connected indoor air-quality sensors for their properties. Her days are a mix of discovery calls, product walkthroughs, proposals and the back-and-forth that comes before a deal is signed. She joins the regular pipeline reviews with the rest of the Sales team and brings in colleagues from engineering or operations when a prospect has detailed technical questions.

## Office

Aisha works out of the Portland office, which is also the company headquarters. She is glad to meet there in person or to set up a video call with colleagues in other offices.

## Reaching her

For anything about a deal she is working on, message her directly or post in the Sales team channel.
