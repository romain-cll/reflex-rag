Naomi Boyle works as Quality Engineer on the Operations team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Quality Engineer, I make sure the sensors our contract manufacturers build match what engineering designed. I write inspection criteria, review test data from the line, investigate returns and work through corrective actions with suppliers when something drifts out of spec.

## Office

I work out of the Denver office.

## Working with me

If you see a unit behaving oddly in the field, send me what you know, even if it is incomplete: the device, the symptom and how it was installed. Patterns show up faster when every report lands in one place.
