## Role

Naomi Boyle works as Customer Success Manager on the Customer Success team. She looks after customers who have connected air-quality sensors from Larkspur Devices running in their buildings, helps them get value from the product, and brings their questions and frustrations back to the product and engineering teams.

## Office

Naomi Boyle works out of the Austin office. She spends much of her time on calls with customers, so the team channel is the best way to leave her a message. If you hear a customer story that deserves attention, she would like to know about it and will happily follow it up.
