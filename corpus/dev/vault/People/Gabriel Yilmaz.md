Gabriel Yilmaz is the Chief Executive Officer of Larkspur Devices and leads the Executive team.

## Role

As Chief Executive Officer, Gabriel sets the overall direction and priorities of the company. Larkspur designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources their manufacturing, and a large part of his job is keeping product, sales and operations pointed the same way. He chairs the Executive team meetings and takes part in the company-wide planning discussions.

## Office

Gabriel works out of the Portland office, the company headquarters.

## Reaching him

For anything that needs his attention, the Executive team is the best first stop. He also reads direct messages, though replies can take a while during busy weeks.
