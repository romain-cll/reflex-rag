## Role

Gabriel Yilmaz is the Chief Executive Officer. He leads Larkspur Devices, the company that designs connected indoor air-quality sensors for commercial buildings and outsources their manufacturing, and he is part of the Executive team that sets the direction for the whole company.

## Office

Gabriel Yilmaz works out of the Portland office, the company headquarters. He likes to hear directly from colleagues in every office and tends to prefer a short message over a long document. If you have something that concerns the whole company, the team channel and the regular company conversations are the right places to raise it.
