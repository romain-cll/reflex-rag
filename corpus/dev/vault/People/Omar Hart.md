Short profile page for Omar Hart, who writes and maintains it himself.

## Role

Omar Hart works as Procurement Specialist on the Operations team. Larkspur Devices outsources manufacturing, so he spends his time with suppliers: asking for quotes, comparing offers, and keeping track of what each vendor has agreed to. He is the first person to ask about the status of a request that involves a supplier.

## Office

Omar Hart works out of the Boston office. He works with the rest of the Operations team, and the team channel is the simplest way to reach him. When you write to him about a supplier, say which part or service you mean so that he can find the right thread quickly.
