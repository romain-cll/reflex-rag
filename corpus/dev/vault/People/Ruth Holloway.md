Ruth Holloway works as Electrical Engineer on the Hardware Engineering team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

I am an Electrical Engineer. My work covers schematics, board layout reviews and the bring-up of new sensor boards, plus the power and signal checks that come before a design goes out to the contract manufacturer. I also help the firmware engineers when a bug turns out to live in the hardware rather than in the code.

## Office

I am based in the Austin office. Feel free to stop by my desk if you want to look at a board together.

## Ask me about

Power budgets, component choices, test fixtures and anything that smells like a grounding problem. If you are filing a hardware issue, a short description of the setup and a photo of the board save us both a round of questions.
