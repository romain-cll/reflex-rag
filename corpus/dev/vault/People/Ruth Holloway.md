Short profile page for Ruth Holloway, who writes and maintains it herself.

## Role

Ruth Holloway works as Electrical Engineer on the Hardware Engineering team. She works on the electronics of the connected air-quality sensors that Larkspur Devices designs, from choosing components to reviewing how a board behaves, and she is a good contact for any question about the circuitry inside a device.

## Office

Ruth Holloway works out of the Austin office. She exchanges ideas with the rest of the hardware group through the team channel and design reviews, and she is glad to explain a design choice to colleagues from other teams. Write to her with a short description of the problem and any photos or notes you have.
