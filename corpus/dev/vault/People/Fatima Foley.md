Fatima Foley works as Head of Customer Success on the Customer Success team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As Head of Customer Success, I run the team that looks after customers once their sensors are installed. We handle onboarding, check-ins, renewals and escalations, and we make sure what customers tell us reaches product and engineering in a form they can act on.

## Office

I work out of the Austin office.

## Working with me

If a customer is unhappy or at risk, I want to hear about it early, even before the details are clear. I also welcome ideas from any team on how we can make installation and day-to-day use smoother for facility staff.
