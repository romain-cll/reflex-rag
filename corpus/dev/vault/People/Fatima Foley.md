## Role

Fatima Foley works as Head of Customer Success on the Customer Success team. She leads the group that looks after customers once their sensors are installed in their buildings, and she is the person to talk to about how the company supports those customers, what they tell us, and how that feedback travels back to the other teams.

## Office

Fatima Foley works out of the Portland office, the company headquarters. She keeps a regular rhythm of conversations with her team and with the product group, and the team channel is the quickest way to reach her. If you are visiting headquarters, she is glad to make time for a chat.
