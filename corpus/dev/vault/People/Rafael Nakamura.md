Short profile page for Rafael Nakamura, who writes and maintains it himself.

## Role

Rafael Nakamura works as Firmware Engineer on the Firmware team. He writes the software that runs on the connected air-quality sensors made by Larkspur Devices, and he is the right contact when a question is about how a device behaves once it is powered on and talking to the network.

## Office

Rafael Nakamura works out of the Chicago office. He collaborates with the hardware engineers and the product managers mostly through the team channel and video calls, so do not hesitate to send him a message. He is happy to explain how a behaviour of the firmware came about, or to point you at the right person if the question belongs elsewhere.
