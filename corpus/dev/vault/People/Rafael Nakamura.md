Rafael Nakamura works as Firmware Engineer on the Firmware team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

I am a Firmware Engineer. I work on the embedded code inside our indoor air-quality sensors: reading the sensing elements, filtering the measurements, managing power and sending data to the cloud. Code reviews, test builds and bench debugging with the hardware engineers fill most of my days.

## Office

I work out of the Portland office.

## Ask me about

Sensor drivers, low-power modes, update packages and the build tooling. When you report a firmware issue, please note the build you were running and the steps that triggered it, so I can reproduce it on my bench.
