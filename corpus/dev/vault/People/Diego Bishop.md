Short profile page for Diego Bishop, who writes and maintains it himself.

## Role

Diego Bishop works as Product Manager on the Product team. He works with engineering, sales and customer success to shape the connected air-quality sensors of Larkspur Devices, writing down what the product should do and keeping track of what customers say about it once it is installed in their buildings.

## Office

Diego Bishop works out of the Austin office. He works with colleagues in other cities mostly through video calls and the team channel, and he welcomes feedback on the product from anyone in the company. A short message that explains the situation is the best way to start a conversation with him.
