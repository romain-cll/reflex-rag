Diego Bishop works as Product Manager on the Product team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Product Manager, I sit between customers, engineering and the go-to-market teams. I listen to what facility teams need from an indoor air-quality sensor, write it down as clear requirements and help the team decide what to build first. I also review release notes and keep the roadmap documents tidy.

## Office

I work out of the Portland office.

## Working with me

I am happy to join a customer call when a feature request needs context. For quick questions, use the team channel. For bigger topics, a short written proposal gets the fastest and most useful answer from me.
