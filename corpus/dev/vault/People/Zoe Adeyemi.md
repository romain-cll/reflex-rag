Zoe Adeyemi works as Electrical Engineer on the Hardware Engineering team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

I am an Electrical Engineer. I design and review the circuits inside our air-quality sensors, from the sensing front end to power and radio, and I help validate boards when samples come back from the manufacturer. Test plans and design reviews take up a good part of my time.

## Office

I am based in the Austin office.

## Ask me about

Component selection, schematic reviews, noise on sensor readings and anything that needs a scope or a multimeter. If you are filing a hardware bug, a photo of the board and a short description of the setup help a lot. I am also happy to walk anyone new through how our boards are put together.
