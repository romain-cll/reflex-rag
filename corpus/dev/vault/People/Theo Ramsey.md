## Role

Theo Ramsey works as Finance Manager on the Finance team. He keeps an eye on how Larkspur Devices spends and plans its money, supports the other teams when they need a budget question answered, and is the person to ask about how finance reviews a request that involves cost.

## Office

Theo Ramsey works out of the Portland office, the company headquarters. His door is usually open for colleagues who want to talk through a budget or a purchase before it goes further. For anything that needs a written record, send him a message on the team channel and include what you are trying to decide.
