Carmen Iverson works as Product Manager on the Product team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Product Manager, I turn what we hear from building operators and facility teams into clear requirements for our indoor air-quality sensors. Most of my week goes to writing and refining specs, sorting the backlog with engineering, and making sure the trade-offs behind each feature are written down where people can find them.

## Office

I work out of the Denver office. If you are passing through, I am happy to grab a desk next to you and talk through a product question in person.

## Working with me

I prefer short written notes over long meetings, and I try to answer questions in the team channel the same day. When something is urgent, a direct message is the fastest way to reach me.
