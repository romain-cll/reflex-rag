## Role

Carmen Iverson works as Product Manager on the Product team. She helps decide what the connected air-quality sensors from Larkspur Devices should do next, gathering input from customers, sales and engineering and turning it into something the teams can build. If you have an idea or a complaint about the product, she wants to hear it.

## Office

Carmen Iverson works out of the Boston office. She works with colleagues in other cities mostly through video calls and the team channel, so a short message with some context is the best way to start a conversation with her.
