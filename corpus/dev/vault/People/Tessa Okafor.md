Tessa Okafor works as Mechanical Engineer on the Hardware Engineering team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Mechanical Engineer, Tessa works on the physical side of the company's connected indoor air-quality sensors: enclosures, airflow around the sensing elements, mounting hardware and the tolerances that make parts fit together. Since Larkspur outsources manufacturing, much of the job is reviewing drawings, samples and feedback from manufacturing partners together with the rest of Hardware Engineering.

## Office

Tessa works out of the Portland office, the company headquarters. When prototypes or samples come in, she is usually the person to find for a first look.

## Reaching her

Questions about mechanical design, fit or enclosure details can go to her directly or to the Hardware Engineering team channel.
