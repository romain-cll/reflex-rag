Hazel Osei works as Product Manager on the Product team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Product Manager, I gather input from customers, customer success and engineering, and turn it into requirements for our connected indoor air-quality sensors. I write specs, keep the backlog in order and help the team agree on what ships now and what waits.

## Office

I am based in the Portland office.

## Working with me

I put this page up early on so people would know what I do. I like decisions written down, so expect me to ask for a short note after a long discussion. Questions in the team channel are always welcome, and I am glad to join a customer call when a request needs more context.
