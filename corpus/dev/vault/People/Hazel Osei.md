Profile page for Hazel Osei at Larkspur Devices, written by Hazel herself.

## Role

Hazel Osei works as Product Manager on the Product team. Colleagues from other teams come to her with questions about what the connected air-quality sensors should do for the buildings that use them, and she tries to keep those conversations tied to what customers actually ask for. She works between customers, engineers and the rest of the company, and she likes to hear about problems early.

## Office

Hazel Osei works out of the Denver office. If you are in the building, stop by her desk; otherwise a message on the team channel is the quickest way to reach her.
