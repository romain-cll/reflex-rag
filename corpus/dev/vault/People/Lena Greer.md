Lena Greer works as Customer Success Manager on the Customer Success team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Customer Success Manager, I look after our customers once their sensors are installed: onboarding calls, regular check-ins, renewals and the occasional escalation when a device stops reporting. I collect feedback from facility managers and pass it along to product and support so it does not get lost.

## Office

I work out of the Austin office. I visit customer sites now and then, so the shared calendar is the best way to see when I am around.

## How to reach me

Message me in the team channel for quick questions. For anything that involves a customer account, add me to the thread early so I have the full picture before the next call.
