Emeka Nordin is the Chief Operating Officer of Larkspur Devices and sits on the Executive team.

## Role

As Chief Operating Officer, I oversee how the company runs day to day: operations, procurement, quality and our relationships with the contract manufacturers that build our sensors. My job is to make sure that what engineering designs and what the sales team commits to can actually be built, shipped and supported.

## Office

I work out of the Portland office, which is also our headquarters.

## Working with me

My door is open for anything that blocks delivery. Before a meeting, I prefer a short written summary with the problem, the options you looked at and what you recommend. That way we can spend the meeting on the decision rather than on the background.
