## Role

Luis Whitaker works as Account Executive on the Sales team. He helps commercial building owners and operators decide whether the connected air-quality sensors of Larkspur Devices fit their needs, and he keeps the product and customer success groups informed about what customers want to see.

## Office

Luis Whitaker works out of the Austin office. When he is not on a customer call, he is easy to reach on the team channel. He likes to compare what he hears from customers with what colleagues in other cities are hearing, and he welcomes a quick conversation about it.
