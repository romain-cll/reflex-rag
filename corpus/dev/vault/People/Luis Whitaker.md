Luis Whitaker works as Account Executive on the Sales team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As an Account Executive, I work with commercial building owners and facility teams who want better visibility into their indoor air quality, from the first conversation through the signed order. In practice that means discovery calls, demos, proposals and a lot of follow-up.

## Office

I am based in the Denver office.

## Working with me

If you hear about a building that is struggling with its air quality, send it my way. I also like to pair with engineering before a technical demo, so the answers we give customers are accurate and we do not promise anything the product cannot do. The team channel is the quickest way to reach me between calls.
