Ingrid Kaplan works as VP of Sales on the Sales team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As VP of Sales, I run the sales organization: the account executives, the pipeline reviews, pricing discussions with finance and the forecast we share with the executive team. I spend a lot of time with key customers, especially when a deal needs an executive in the room.

## Office

I am based in the Portland office.

## Working with me

I like to see deals early, while there is still time to shape them. Bring me the customer's problem, not only the price, and loop in engineering whenever a buyer asks a technical question we cannot answer on the spot.
