## Role

Ingrid Kaplan works as VP of Sales on the Sales team. She leads the group of account executives who sell the connected air-quality sensors of Larkspur Devices to commercial buildings, and she speaks for the sales side when the leadership discusses customers, pipeline and priorities.

## Office

Ingrid Kaplan works out of the Boston office. She is in frequent contact with her team in the other cities through video calls and the team channel, and she makes a point of joining customer conversations when a deal needs another voice. For questions about how sales works with the rest of the company, send her a message with some context.
