Short profile page for Claire Gallagher, who writes and maintains it herself.

## Role

Claire Gallagher works as Quality Engineer on the Operations team. Larkspur Devices outsources manufacturing, so much of her work is about making sure the sensors that come back from the contract manufacturers meet the standard the company expects. When a unit misbehaves in the field, she is one of the people who helps work out why.

## Office

Claire Gallagher works out of the Chicago office. She works closely with procurement and with the hardware engineers, and the team channel is the best way to reach her. If you have a quality concern, describe what you saw as precisely as you can and she will take it from there.
