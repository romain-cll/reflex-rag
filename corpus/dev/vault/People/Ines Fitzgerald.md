Ines Fitzgerald works as Procurement Specialist on the Operations team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

As a Procurement Specialist, I handle purchase orders, supplier follow-ups and the paperwork that keeps parts flowing to our contract manufacturers. I compare offers, track lead times and flag shortages before they turn into delays on the line.

## Office

I am based in the Portland office.

## How I can help

If you need a component sourced, a quote requested or an order chased, send me the part details and the timing you need. I keep the supplier contact list current, so I can usually point you to the right person on the vendor side. For anything that changes a part we already buy, please loop me in early.
