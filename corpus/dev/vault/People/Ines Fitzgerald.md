Profile page for Ines Fitzgerald at Larkspur Devices, written by Ines herself.

## Role

Ines Fitzgerald works as Customer Success Manager on the Customer Success team. She is the person customers call when a deployment of sensors in their buildings raises questions, and she passes what she hears back to the product and engineering teams. She cares about customers getting a clear answer quickly and about the right colleague seeing their feedback.

## Office

Ines Fitzgerald works out of the Denver office. The easiest way to get her attention is a note on the team channel, and she is usually glad to talk through a customer story over coffee.
