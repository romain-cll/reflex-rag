Hector Haddad works as Firmware Engineer on the Firmware team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

I write and maintain the code that runs on our sensors: drivers for the air-quality sensing elements, the connectivity stack, power management and over-the-air updates. As a Firmware Engineer, I also spend a fair amount of time on the bench with the hardware team, chasing down issues that only show up on real devices.

## Office

I work out of the Denver office.

## About this page

I put this page together early on so people would know where to find me. For firmware questions, the team channel is the quickest route. For anything about a specific build, please include the version you ran and the device you tested on, which saves a lot of back and forth.
