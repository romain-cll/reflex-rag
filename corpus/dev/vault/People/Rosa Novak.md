Rosa Novak works as Mechanical Engineer on the Hardware Engineering team at Larkspur Devices.

## Role

I am a Mechanical Engineer. I design the enclosures and mounting hardware for our indoor air-quality sensors, with a lot of attention on airflow, ease of installation and parts that mold cleanly. I also review tooling and first samples with our manufacturing partners.

## Office

I am based in the Austin office.

I put this page together early on. For enclosure or fit questions, send me a photo and a short description, which is usually quicker than a meeting. I am always happy to look at a prototype on the bench with anyone who is curious.
