Vantage Electronics sent its quote for the Atlas circuit board assembly. The offer answers the circuit board assembly section of [[Atlas requirements]], which asks for pricing per board. Background on the supplier is on the [[Vantage Electronics]] page.

## Scope

Assembly of the Atlas circuit board, including placement of components and basic testing of each assembled board before shipment. Component sourcing terms are to be clarified with the supplier.

## Price

Vantage Electronics quoted $12.70 per board for the Atlas circuit board assembly. The price is per assembled board, as the requirements ask.

## Lead time

Lead time is to be confirmed with the supplier, both for engineering builds and for production.

## Conditions

- Pricing assumes the board design is stable when the first build is ordered.
- Payment terms and volume commitments are still to be confirmed with the supplier.

## Procurement notes

The per-board price is in a usable form for the cost rollup. Next step is to share it with Hardware Engineering and the product owner so it can be checked against the unit cost target.
