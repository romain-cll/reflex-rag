Cobalt Molding sent its quote for the Atlas enclosure tooling. The offer answers the enclosure section of [[Atlas requirements]] and was received by procurement for comparison with the other enclosure offers. Background on the supplier is on the [[Cobalt Molding]] page.

## Scope

Tooling for the Atlas enclosure, built to the drawings and the enclosure section of the requirements. Part pricing for production runs is not part of this offer and will be handled separately.

## Price

Cobalt Molding quoted $55,500 for the Atlas enclosure tooling. This is a one-time cost.

## Lead time

Cobalt Molding needs 11 weeks to deliver the Atlas enclosure tooling.

## Conditions

- The offer assumes the enclosure design is frozen before tooling work starts. Later changes would be reviewed with the supplier.
- Payment terms, tooling ownership and sample rounds are still to be confirmed with the supplier.

## Procurement notes

The offer is clear and complete enough to compare. Next step is to put it side by side with the other enclosure offer and review both with Hardware Engineering against the enclosure requirements.
