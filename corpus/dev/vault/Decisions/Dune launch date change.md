## Context

The launch date of Dune has to change. The reasons are not repeated here: they are in the schedule review, [[2025-12-03 Dune schedule review]], which went through what moved and why.

## Decision

Dune will now launch on May 24, 2027. This replaces the previous launch date, and the decision is made by Carmen Iverson as the owner of the project.

## Consequences

Everyone working on Dune plans against the new launch date from now on. Work that was scheduled around the previous date is reviewed against the new one, and any message to customers or partners that mentions the launch uses the new date. The team keeps the schedule review as the place to look for the reasoning behind the change.
