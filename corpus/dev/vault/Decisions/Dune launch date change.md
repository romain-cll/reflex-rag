## Context

Dune had a launch date set at the kickoff. A certification test now has to be run again, and the product cannot ship before the retest is complete and its report is available. The original date no longer holds. The full reasons, and the discussion of the options, are in the [[2025-12-12 Dune schedule review]].

## Decision

Dune will now launch on August 2, 2026. Carmen Iverson made the decision as product owner, after the schedule review.

## Consequences

- The previous launch date no longer applies. Any page or plan that still shows it is out of date and should be read with this note in mind.
- Milestones leading up to launch move to match the new date.
- The new date is shared with the wider team and with the pilot customer.
- Further changes to the launch date go through a new decision note.
