## Context

The launch date of Atlas has changed. The team reviewed the schedule and concluded that the original launch date no longer holds. The reasons are in the schedule review: see [[2025-08-01 Atlas schedule review]] for the full account.

## Decision

Atlas will now launch on July 8, 2027. Hazel Osei, as the owner of the project, decided on this date and records it here.

## Consequences

- The new date replaces the original launch date wherever the latter appears.
- Plans and commitments that were built on the original date should be read again against the new one.
- Anyone who needs the background should read the schedule review rather than this record, which only states the outcome.
