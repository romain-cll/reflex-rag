## Context

The original Atlas launch date could not hold. The schedule was reviewed with the people closest to the problem, and the reasons for the change, along with the milestones that move with it, are in the [[2025-07-19 Atlas schedule review]].

## Decision

Atlas will now launch on February 18, 2026. This replaces the original launch date, which should no longer be used for planning.

The decision was made by Hazel Osei as product owner of Atlas.

## Consequences

- Every team working on Atlas plans against the new launch date from now on.
- Any page, plan or customer conversation that still mentions the original date is out of date and should be corrected when it comes up.
- Milestones that depend on the launch are reworked around the new date.
- If the new date comes under pressure again, it goes back to the product owner before anything else changes.
