This note records the new launch date of Fjord. It is a short record: the reasons behind the change are left to the schedule review.

## Context

The previous launch date for Fjord no longer holds. The reasons are in [[2026-04-20 Fjord schedule review]], which is the place to look for what changed and why. This record only fixes the outcome so that anyone planning around Fjord has one date to work with.

## Decision

Diego Bishop decided that Fjord will now launch on February 26, 2027. This date replaces the original launch date for planning purposes.

## Consequences

Every plan that was built around the original launch date has to be read against the new one. The owners of those plans are responsible for adjusting them, and questions about the date go to the product owner.
