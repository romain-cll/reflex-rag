## Context

The original Fjord launch date could not hold. The schedule was reviewed with the people closest to the problem, and the reasons for the change, along with the milestones that move with it, are in the [[2026-04-19 Fjord schedule review]].

## Decision

Fjord will now launch on November 17, 2026. This replaces the original launch date, which should no longer be used for planning.

The decision was made by Diego Bishop as product owner of Fjord.

## Consequences

- Every team working on Fjord plans against the new launch date from now on.
- Any page, plan or customer conversation that still mentions the original date is out of date and should be corrected when it comes up.
- Milestones that depend on the launch are reworked around the new date.
- If the new date comes under pressure again, it goes back to the product owner before anything else changes.
