## Context

The launch date of Ember had to be revisited after the schedule review. The reasons for the change are in [[2026-03-23 Ember schedule review]], and they are not repeated here so that this record stays short and has a single purpose: to state the new date.

## Decision

Ember will now launch on March 16, 2027. Hazel Osei, as the product owner, made the decision, and it replaces the original launch date from the moment this record is written.

## Consequences

Every plan that depends on the launch date follows the new one. The teams involved in Ember adjust their own schedules against it, and procurement and quality check that their commitments still fit. Anyone looking for the original date should treat this record as the reference for the current one. Questions about the change go to the product owner.
