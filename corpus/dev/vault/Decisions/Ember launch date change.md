## Context

The Ember launch date had to be revisited. The reasons, and the discussion that led to the new date, are in the [[2026-02-23 Ember schedule review]].

## Decision

Ember will now launch on October 24, 2026. This replaces the original launch date set at the project kickoff. Hazel Osei made the decision as product owner of Ember, after the schedule review with operations.

## Consequences

- Plans that depend on the launch, such as production, sales and marketing timing, move to the new date.
- Any page or document that still shows the original launch date is out of date and should be corrected by its owner.
- Teams should plan against the new date from now on and raise any conflict with the product owner.
