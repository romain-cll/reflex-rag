## Context

At the [[2026-07-17 Granite schedule review]], the team found that the original launch date for Granite could no longer hold. The reasons for the slip, and the discussion that led to a new date, are set out in that schedule review and are not repeated here.

## Decision

Granite will now launch on February 24, 2027. Carmen Iverson took the decision, and this record replaces the original launch date as the reference for the project.

## Consequences

- Every plan built on the original launch date has to be reviewed against the new one. Each team owns its own plan and flags anything that no longer fits.
- Pages and documents that still show the original date should be corrected as they are touched, starting with the ones customers or partners see.
- Any further change to the launch date goes through the same process: a schedule review first, then a new decision record.
