## Context

The launch schedule of Granite had to change. The reasons are in the schedule review, [[2026-07-05 Granite schedule review]], and this record does not repeat them.

## Decision

Granite will now launch on June 26, 2027. Carmen Iverson, as the owner of the project, made the decision.

## Consequences

Anyone planning work around the launch of Granite should use this date from now on. Plans that were built on the earlier date need to be looked at again by the teams that own them. Questions about why the launch moved should be directed to the schedule review.
