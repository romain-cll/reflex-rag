## Context

The schedule of Cirrus was reviewed with the people who are closest to it, and the launch date had to move. The reasons are left to the schedule review, [[2025-10-26 Cirrus schedule review]], so this record stays short and only carries the outcome.

## Decision

Cirrus will now launch on June 27, 2027. This is the launch date the team plans against from now on. Diego Bishop made the decision as the owner of the project.

## Consequences

The new date replaces the earlier launch date for planning purposes. Anyone who organizes work around the launch, whether on the product, the quality side or the customer side, should use the new date. The team will keep following the schedule as it develops and raise any further change with the owner of the project.
