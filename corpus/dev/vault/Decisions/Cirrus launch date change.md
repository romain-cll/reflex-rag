## Context

The Cirrus launch can no longer hold its original date. The schedule was reviewed with operations and quality, and the reasons for the change are laid out in [[2025-09-12 Cirrus schedule review]].

## Decision

Cirrus will now launch on April 8, 2026. This date replaces the original launch date, and Diego Bishop, as product owner of Cirrus, owns it.

## Consequences

- All Cirrus planning now works against April 8, 2026. Any older note or page that shows a different launch date is out of date.
- Teams that depend on the launch should check their own plans against the new date and raise conflicts with the product owner.
- Any further risk to the date is reported to the product owner as soon as it is known, rather than at the next review.
