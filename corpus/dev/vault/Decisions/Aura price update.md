## Context

This record covers a change to the list price of the Larkspur Aura product line. Pricing for the line is reviewed from time to time, and this note keeps the outcome in one place so that sales, finance and anyone preparing customer material can find it.

## Decision

Ingrid Kaplan, as VP of Sales, decided that the list price of the Larkspur Aura rises to $369 on June 14, 2026. The change was approved by the CEO, [[Gabriel Yilmaz]].

## Consequences

- From that date, the sales team works from the new list price for the Larkspur Aura.
- Any price sheet, presentation or web page that shows the list price of the Aura needs to carry the new figure from that date.
- Questions about the change go to the VP of Sales.
