## Context

This record covers a change to the list price of the Aura product line. It is written down so that everyone who quotes, publishes or answers questions about the price of the Larkspur Aura works from the same number.

## Decision

The list price of the Larkspur Aura rises to $239 on June 1, 2026. Ingrid Kaplan made the decision for Sales, and the change was approved by the CEO, [[Gabriel Yilmaz]].

## Consequences

- From June 1, 2026, the Larkspur Aura is listed at $239. Price lists and sales materials that show the Aura list price need to carry the new price by then.
- Account owners should let their customers know about the change ahead of time, so that nobody learns about it from an invoice.
- The sales team should use the new list price in anything prepared for dates from June 1, 2026 onward.
- Questions about the new price go to Ingrid Kaplan.
