## Overview

Foxborough Campus Services is a corporate campus customer based in Spokane. It uses Larkspur's connected indoor air-quality sensors in the buildings of its campus. This page was set up when the deal closed and is meant as the quick reference for the account.

## Main contact

Yuki Keane, Facilities Manager, is the main contact at Foxborough Campus Services. Yuki coordinates access to the buildings and relays questions from the facilities team, so operational topics should go through Yuki rather than straight to the site staff.

## Account owner

Anya Castillo owns the Foxborough Campus Services account. [[Anya Castillo]] owns the relationship with the customer and handles commercial conversations, contract questions and escalations. Anyone at Larkspur who needs to reach the customer should check with her first.

## Contract

Foxborough Campus Services has an annual contract worth $63,000. Invoicing runs through finance, and any change to the scope of the agreement goes through the account owner.
