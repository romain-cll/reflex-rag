Larchmont College is a university customer based in Columbus. This page was set up when the deal closed and holds the reference details for the account.

## Overview

The college uses Larkspur air-quality sensors to keep an eye on indoor conditions in its campus buildings. Like other higher-education customers, it wants dashboards that facilities staff can read at a glance and reports that are easy to share with campus leadership. Work on site is easier to schedule around the academic calendar, so plan visits with the main contact well ahead.

## Main contact

Kenji Weller, Facilities Manager, is the main contact at Larchmont College. He handles building access, coordinates with the maintenance crews and collects feedback from the people who use the readings. Technical and operational questions should go through him.

## Account owner

Aisha Whitfield owns the Larchmont College account. Reach out to [[Aisha Whitfield]], who owns the relationship, before contacting the college about commercial topics, pricing questions or escalations.
