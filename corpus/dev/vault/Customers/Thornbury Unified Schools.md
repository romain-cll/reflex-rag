Thornbury Unified Schools is a school district customer based in Baltimore. The district uses Larkspur's connected indoor air-quality sensors to keep an eye on conditions in its school buildings. This page was set up when the deal closed and holds the basics of the account.

## Overview

For a school district, the interest is in healthy classrooms and in giving the facilities team readings they can act on and share with school leaders and parents when questions come up. Most of the day-to-day contact runs through the district's facilities side.

## Contract

Thornbury Unified Schools has an annual contract worth $136,000. Invoicing and contract paperwork are handled by finance; the account owner is the point of contact for any commercial question.
