Prescott Regional Hospital is a hospital customer based in Madison. This page was written when the deal closed and holds the reference details for the account.

## Overview

The hospital uses Larkspur air-quality sensors to monitor indoor conditions across its facilities. Like other healthcare customers, it expects dependable readings, clear reporting for its facilities team and fast answers from support. Access to clinical areas is tightly controlled, so every site visit has to be planned with the main contact in advance.

## Main contact

Dana Ortega, Facilities Director, is the main contact at Prescott Regional Hospital. She approves access for installation and maintenance work, coordinates with her facilities staff and collects their feedback on the readings. Operational and technical questions should go through her.

## Account owner

Aisha Whitfield owns the Prescott Regional Hospital account. Check with [[Aisha Whitfield]], who owns the relationship, before contacting the hospital about commercial topics, pricing questions or escalations.
