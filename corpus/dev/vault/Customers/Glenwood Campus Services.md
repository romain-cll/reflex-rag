Glenwood Campus Services is a corporate campus customer based in Sacramento. This page was created when the deal closed and keeps the reference details for the account together.

## Overview

The customer runs the facilities of a corporate campus and uses Larkspur air-quality sensors to follow indoor conditions in its office buildings. Its priorities are simple: dependable readings, reports that building managers can act on and a clear path to support when a sensor needs attention.

## Main contact

Amara Lowell, Facilities Director, is the main contact at Glenwood Campus Services. She is the day-to-day counterpart for Larkspur on the customer side, approves access for installation work and gathers feedback from her building managers. Operational questions should go through her.
