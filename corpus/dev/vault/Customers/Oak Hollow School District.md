## Overview

Oak Hollow School District is a school district customer based in Madison. The district uses Larkspur's connected indoor air-quality sensors in its school buildings. This page was written when the deal closed and serves as the reference for the account.

## Main contact

Joanna Delacroix, Facilities Manager, is the main contact at Oak Hollow School District. Joanna is the one to ask about access to the schools, scheduling work around the school calendar, and how the facilities team uses the readings. Requests from Larkspur to the district should go through her.

## Account owner

Aisha Whitfield owns the Oak Hollow School District account. [[Aisha Whitfield]] owns the relationship with the district and takes care of commercial conversations, contract questions and escalations. Colleagues who need to contact the district should check with her first.

## Contract

Oak Hollow School District has an annual contract worth $35,000. Invoicing and payment questions go to finance. Any change to the scope of the agreement should be discussed with the account owner before it is raised with the district.
