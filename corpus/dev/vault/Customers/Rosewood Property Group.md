Rosewood Property Group is a property management customer based in Eugene. This page was written when the deal closed and gathers the reference details for the account.

## Overview

The group manages a portfolio of commercial buildings and uses Larkspur air-quality sensors to give its building teams and tenants a clear picture of indoor conditions. As with most property managers, it cares about reports it can share with tenants, dependable readings and fast answers from support when a sensor needs attention.

## Main contact

Theo Pruitt, Head of Building Services, is the main contact at Rosewood Property Group. He coordinates access to the buildings, works with the on-site staff and collects feedback from the property managers. Operational and technical questions should go through him.

## Account owner

Anya Castillo owns the Rosewood Property Group account. Talk to [[Anya Castillo]], who owns the relationship, before contacting the customer about commercial topics, pricing questions or escalations.
