## Overview

Meadowbrook College is a university customer based in Salt Lake City. The college uses Larkspur's connected indoor air-quality sensors in its campus buildings. This page was written when the deal closed and gathers the reference details anyone working on the account will need.

## Main contact

Tariq Mills, Head of Building Services, is the main contact at Meadowbrook College. He is the right person for questions about building access, installation logistics and how his team uses the readings day to day. Keep him in the loop on anything that touches the buildings he looks after.

## Account owner

Tomas Mbeki owns the Meadowbrook College account. Commercial questions, contract matters and escalations from the college go through [[Tomas Mbeki]], who owns the relationship on the Larkspur side. If someone else on the team needs to reach the customer, check with him first so the college hears one consistent voice.

## Contract

Meadowbrook College has an annual contract worth $174,000. Billing and invoicing questions go to finance. Anything that would change the scope of the agreement should be raised with the account owner before it reaches the customer.
