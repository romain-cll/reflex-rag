## Overview

Westgate Property Group is a property management customer based in Pittsburgh. It uses Larkspur's connected indoor air-quality sensors in the buildings it manages. This page was written when the deal closed and holds the basics for anyone who works on the account.

## Main contact

Kofi Sorensen, Facilities Director, is the main contact at Westgate Property Group. Kofi is the person to talk to about building access, tenant-facing questions and how the facilities team uses the readings. Operational requests should go through him so his team is not contacted from several directions at once.

## Account owner

Luis Whitaker owns the Westgate Property Group account. [[Luis Whitaker]] owns the relationship with the customer and handles commercial discussions, contract questions and escalations. Colleagues who need to reach the customer should check with him first.

## Contract

Westgate Property Group has an annual contract worth $131,000. Invoices and payment questions are handled by finance. Any request that would change the scope of the agreement should go to the account owner before anything is promised to the customer.
