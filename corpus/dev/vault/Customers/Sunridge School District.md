Sunridge School District is a school district customer based in Eugene. This page was written when the deal closed and keeps the reference details for the account.

## Overview

The district uses Larkspur air-quality sensors to follow indoor conditions in its school buildings. Like most school customers, it wants readings that building staff can trust, simple reports it can share with school leaders and parents, and quick answers from support. Installation work and site visits are easier to schedule outside of class time, so plan them with the main contact early.

## Main contact

Grace Mercer, Head of Building Services, is the main contact at Sunridge School District. She coordinates access to the schools, works with the custodial and maintenance staff and gathers feedback from the buildings. Operational questions should go through her.

## Account owner

Luis Whitaker owns the Sunridge School District account. [[Luis Whitaker]] owns the relationship, so check with him before contacting the district about commercial topics, pricing questions or escalations.

## Contract

Sunridge School District has an annual contract worth $57,000. Finance handles invoicing and billing questions, and the account owner keeps the signed documents.
