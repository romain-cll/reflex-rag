Granite is the project behind the Larkspur Pulse Gen 2. It is owned by Carmen Iverson, and its status is active.

## Summary

Granite brings together the product, hardware engineering and operations work needed to take the Larkspur Pulse Gen 2 from design to customers. This page is the place to find the current status, the key dates and the main documents of the project. The project started with [[2025-10-25 Granite kickoff]].

## Requirements

The requirements for power, cost, the enclosure and the battery pack are written down in [[Granite requirements]]. Engineering and procurement work from that document when they make design choices and ask suppliers for offers.

## Enclosure

The team chose to move forward with Wexford Molding for the Granite enclosure. The vendor choice was discussed in [[2025-12-04 Granite enclosure review]], where the team compared the offers it had received and checked them against the requirements.

## Key dates

Granite is scheduled to launch on April 15, 2027.

## Team

Carmen Iverson owns the project and keeps this page. Hardware engineering and procurement work with her on the design and on the suppliers.
