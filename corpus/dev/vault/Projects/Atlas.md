## Summary

Atlas is the project behind the Larkspur Aura Gen 2, and Hazel Osei owns it. The project started at [[2025-01-09 Atlas kickoff]], where the goal, the owner and the target launch date were agreed.

## Status

The project is active. The requirements for the hardware are written in [[Atlas requirements]]. For the enclosure, the team chose to move forward with Ironwood Plastics, and the way the vendor was picked is described in [[2025-02-18 Atlas enclosure review]].

## Key dates

- Launch: Atlas is scheduled to launch on April 9, 2027.
- Enclosure vendor: Ironwood Plastics.
- Page last updated: 2025-02-23.

## Team

Hazel Osei is the product owner. Hardware engineering covers the mechanical and electrical design, and procurement handles the suppliers.
