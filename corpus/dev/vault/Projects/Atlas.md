## Summary

Atlas is the project to build a low-cost sensor for open-plan offices, sold as the Larkspur Aura Gen 2. Hazel Osei is the product owner. The goal, the owner and the launch date were set at the [[2025-01-25 Atlas kickoff]], which is where the project started.

## Status

Active. The requirements for power, cost and the enclosure are in [[Atlas requirements]], which is the approved reference for the team. Supplier offers for the enclosure tooling and the circuit board assembly have come in and are being worked through with procurement.

The team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics for the Atlas enclosure. The comparison of the two enclosure offers and the reasons for the choice are in the [[2025-03-06 Atlas enclosure review]].

## Key dates

- Kickoff: 2025-01-25.
- Launch: Atlas is scheduled to launch on December 9, 2025.

## Suppliers

- Enclosure: Pacifica Plastics.
- Circuit board assembly: offer received, under review.
