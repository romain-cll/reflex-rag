## Summary

Fjord is the project to build a sensor kit for school gyms and auditoriums, sold as the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2. Diego Bishop is the product owner. The goal, the owner and the launch date were set at the [[2025-10-26 Fjord kickoff]], which is where the project started.

## Status

Active. The targets for power, cost, the enclosure and the sensor module are in [[Fjord requirements]], the approved reference for the team. Tooling offers for the enclosure and an offer for the sensor module have come in and are being worked through with procurement.

The team chose to move forward with Cobalt Molding for the Fjord enclosure. The comparison of the two enclosure offers and the reasoning behind the choice are in the [[2025-12-05 Fjord enclosure review]].

## Key dates

- Kickoff: 2025-10-26.
- Launch: Fjord is scheduled to launch on August 28, 2026.

## Suppliers

- Enclosure: Cobalt Molding.
- Sensor module: offer received, under review.
