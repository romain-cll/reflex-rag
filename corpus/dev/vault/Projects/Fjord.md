This is the hub page of Fjord, the project behind the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2. Diego Bishop owns it and keeps this page as the place to find the status, the key dates and the main documents.

## Summary

Fjord is a hardware project of the Product team and the Hardware Engineering team, with procurement helping on the outside suppliers. The product is built by an outside manufacturer, as for the rest of the Larkspur line.

## Status

The project is active. The team chose to move forward with Ironwood Plastics for the Fjord enclosure. The requirements are approved and are the reference for the design.

## Key dates

Fjord is scheduled to launch on December 14, 2026. The rest of the plan is built around that date.
