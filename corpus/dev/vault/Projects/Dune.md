Dune is the project to build a ceiling-mounted sensor for retrofit projects, sold as the Larkspur Halo Gen 2. Carmen Iverson is the product owner and keeps this page current.

## Summary

The goal is a sensor that building operators can add to existing spaces with as little installation work as possible. The scope, the owner and the target date were set at the [[2025-06-20 Dune kickoff]], which is where the project started. The power, cost and enclosure targets are written down in [[Dune requirements]], the reference document for the whole team.

## Status

Active. The requirements are approved and vendor work is under way with procurement.

For the enclosure, the team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics for the Dune enclosure. The comparison of the two enclosure offers, and the reasons for the choice, are in the [[2025-07-30 Dune enclosure review]]. Battery pack sourcing is being handled by procurement.

## Key dates

- Kickoff: 2025-06-20.
- Enclosure vendor review: 2025-07-30.
- Launch: Dune is scheduled to launch on May 4, 2026.
