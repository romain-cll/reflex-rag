## Summary

Dune is the project behind the Larkspur Halo Gen 2. Carmen Iverson owns it, and the work involves the product, hardware engineering and operations teams of Larkspur Devices. This page is the hub for the project: it gives the status, the key dates and the links to the notes that matter.

## Status

The project is active. It started with the kickoff meeting recorded in [[2025-05-06 Dune kickoff]], and the product requirements are written up in [[Dune requirements]]. The enclosure vendor was discussed in [[2025-06-15 Dune enclosure review]], and the team chose to move forward with Lattice Molding for the Dune enclosure.

## Key dates

- Dune is scheduled to launch on March 6, 2027.

## Enclosure

Lattice Molding is the enclosure vendor for Dune. Questions about the enclosure go to mechanical engineering, and questions about the vendor relationship go to procurement.
