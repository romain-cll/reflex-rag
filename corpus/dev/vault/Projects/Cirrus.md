Cirrus is the project behind the Larkspur Vista Gen 2. It is owned by Diego Bishop, and this page is the hub for everything written about it: the kickoff, the requirements, the enclosure vendor and the launch date.

## Summary

The project started with the kickoff meeting, [[2025-03-15 Cirrus kickoff]], where the team agreed on the goal, the owner and the launch plan. The requirements for power, cost, the enclosure and the sensor module are written down in [[Cirrus requirements]].

For the enclosure, the vendor was discussed in [[2025-04-24 Cirrus enclosure review]]. The team chose to move forward with Wexford Molding for the Cirrus enclosure.

## Status

The project is active. The enclosure vendor is Wexford Molding, and the other open items of the project are tracked on this page as they come up.

## Key dates

Cirrus is scheduled to launch on March 31, 2027. The team plans its work backwards from that date.
