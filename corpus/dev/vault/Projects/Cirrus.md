Cirrus is the project behind the Larkspur Vista Gen 2. Diego Bishop owns it as product owner.

## Summary

The project started at [[2025-03-21 Cirrus kickoff]], where the team agreed on the goal, the owner and the launch target. The requirements, with their power, cost, sensor module and enclosure sections, are in [[Cirrus requirements]].

## Status

Active. The requirements are approved, and supplier offers are in for the enclosure tooling and the sensor module. Hardware is working through the enclosure drawings with the vendor, and the sensor module offer is being checked against the requirements and the unit cost target.

Questions about scope go to Diego Bishop. Questions about suppliers go to procurement.

## Enclosure

The team chose to move forward with Juno Plastics for the Cirrus enclosure. The comparison of the two tooling offers, and the reasons for going with Juno Plastics, are in [[2025-04-30 Cirrus enclosure review]].

## Key dates

- Kickoff: 2025-03-21.
- Enclosure vendor review: 2025-04-30.
- Launch: Cirrus is scheduled to launch on January 16, 2026.
