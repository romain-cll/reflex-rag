Ember is the project behind the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2. It is owned by Hazel Osei of the Product team, with the hardware engineers and procurement working alongside her. The status is active.

## Summary

This page is the hub of the project: where it stands, the dates that matter, and the notes to read for more detail. The project started at [[2025-07-06 Ember kickoff]], where the team agreed on its goal and its owner.

## Key dates

Ember is scheduled to launch on January 4, 2027. The team plans the build phases and the supplier work backward from that date.

## Requirements

What the product has to meet, from battery life to cost, the enclosure and the circuit board assembly, is written down in [[Ember requirements]]. Supplier offers are checked against that document.

## Enclosure

The team chose to move forward with Redstone Plastics for the Ember enclosure. Redstone Plastics is the enclosure vendor for the project. The vendor was discussed in [[2025-08-15 Ember enclosure review]], where the two tooling offers were compared.

## Status

The project is active. Questions go to the product owner first.
