## Summary

Ember is the project to build a gateway that collects readings across a multi-floor building. It will be sold as the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2, and Hazel Osei owns the project as product owner. The project started at the [[2025-09-01 Ember kickoff]], where the team agreed on the goal, the owner and the launch date.

## Status

Active. The targets for power, cost, the enclosure and circuit board assembly are set in [[Ember requirements]]. Procurement has collected offers for the enclosure tooling and for the board assembly. For the enclosure, the team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics; the comparison of the two tooling offers is in [[2025-10-11 Ember enclosure review]].

## Key dates

- Kickoff: September 1, 2025.
- Enclosure vendor review: October 11, 2025.
- Launch: Ember is scheduled to launch on July 26, 2026.
