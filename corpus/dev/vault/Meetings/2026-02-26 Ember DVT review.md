Claire Gallagher, Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor and Hazel Osei met for the Ember design validation review. The goal was to go through the results on the DVT units and name the main issue found.

## Discussion

The main issue found on the DVT units is a drift in the CO2 reading. The Ember DVT units showed a CO2 reading drift in warm rooms. Claire presented the test records from the quality side, and the group went through them unit by unit. Ruth, who knows the electronics best, said the sensing path is the first place to look, but that it is too early to say where the cause sits. Tessa wanted to know whether the housing plays any role in how heat reaches the sensor, and the group agreed that it cannot be ruled out yet.

Hazel asked what the finding means for the people already using the product. Some of the DVT units also went to the pilot described in [[2026-02-16 Ember pilot kickoff]], so the team has to keep that in mind when it follows up.

The group closed the review by agreeing that the drift is the topic to follow, and that the rest of the discussion should wait for what the investigation brings.

## Decisions

- The reading drift in warm rooms is the main open issue on the DVT units.

## Action items

- Claire to send a recap of the review to the group.
- Ruth and Tessa to look into the cause from the electronics and housing sides.
- Hazel to keep the pilot customer informed as the investigation moves forward.
