Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Diego Bishop met for the design review of Cirrus. I took the notes.

## Discussion

Most of the time went to a single question: should Cirrus get an e-ink display? Diego described the idea as a way to let people in a meeting room read the state of the air at a glance without opening an app. Tessa pointed out that a display means an opening in the housing, which touches the enclosure design. I raised the power side, since the display and the circuitry behind it have to be weighed against the battery life target. Rafael wondered how often people in a room would actually look at it.

The conversation went around several times. Nobody argued that the idea was a bad one, but nobody had enough information to settle it either: the benefit for customers is hard to put a value on, and the effect on cost, power and the enclosure is only partly understood. We agreed that every side had made a fair point and that we were not going to resolve it in this session.

Apart from the display, the rest of the design review was routine and nothing else needed the group's attention.

## Decisions

The team discussed adding an e-ink display for Cirrus but made no decision. The question stays open. Open questions are tracked on the project page, [[Cirrus]].
