Rosa, Hazel, Ines and Naomi met for the vendor validation of the Atlas enclosure now that the drop tests are done, and to give the tooling sign-off.

## Discussion

Naomi presented the drop test results on the enclosure parts and how they were run. The group went through them against the enclosure section of [[Atlas requirements]]: protection of the electronics, mounting, vents and battery access. Rosa added her own observations from handling the tested parts.

With the drop tests behind them, the group was ready to validate a vendor. The team gave Cobalt Molding the tooling go-ahead for the Atlas enclosure. This sign-off replaces the direction taken at the earlier enclosure review, and it settles the enclosure section of the requirements.

Ines confirmed that the commercial side is ready to move as soon as the go-ahead is sent, and that she will keep procurement copied on everything with the supplier from here. Hazel asked that the outcome be written into the requirements so nobody works from an older assumption. Supplier details and contacts are on the [[Cobalt Molding]] page, and Cobalt Molding is now the validated vendor for the enclosure.

## Decisions

- Cobalt Molding received the tooling go-ahead for the Atlas enclosure after the drop tests.
- Cobalt Molding is the validated enclosure vendor for Atlas.
- The enclosure section of the requirements is settled.
