Emeka Nordin met with Clara Zielinski and Fatima Foley for the monthly operations review.

## Discussion

The group went through the standing items of the review first. None of them needed more than a quick status check, and the open points from the last review were checked off one by one.

Most of the meeting went to one proposal: opening a second warehouse in Reno. Emeka framed it as a question for the leadership team to explore, not something to settle on the spot.

Clara Zielinski asked what a second site would add compared with the current setup, and what it would take to run two locations without losing track of stock. Fatima Foley raised the practical side: staffing, moving inventory, and how orders would be split between the sites.

The discussion went back and forth. There was real interest in the idea, but also a shared sense that the group did not yet have what it needed to weigh it properly. Nobody pushed for a yes or a no in this meeting, and the leadership team made no decision on the second warehouse.

## Decisions

- None. The second warehouse in Reno was discussed, and the proposal stays open.
