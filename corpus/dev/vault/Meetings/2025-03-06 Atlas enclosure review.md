Rosa, Hazel and Ines met to compare the two tooling quotes for the Atlas enclosure and agree on which vendor to move forward with.

## Discussion

Ines walked the group through both offers. The first is [[Atlas enclosure - Pacifica Plastics]], the second is [[Atlas enclosure - Cobalt Molding]]. Both cover tooling for the full enclosure and both assume a frozen design before work starts, so the scope lines up well enough for a direct comparison.

Rosa went through the enclosure section of [[Atlas requirements]] with both offers in hand: mounting, vents, battery access and drop testing. Neither offer raised a question that would rule a vendor out at this stage. She flagged that the real check will come once parts can be tested, and that she wants drop test results before anyone treats the enclosure as finished.

Price carried most of the weight. Pacifica Plastics is clearly the cheaper of the two tooling offers, which matters for a product built around a low-cost target. Cobalt Molding is a little faster on lead time, but the group did not think the difference was enough to make up for the price gap. Hazel asked whether the schedule could absorb the slightly longer lead time; Ines thinks it can if the order goes out soon.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics for the Atlas enclosure, mainly on price.
