Diego, Naomi and Emeka Nordin met to review the Cirrus schedule, now that a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

Naomi explained where certification stands. A certification test on Cirrus has to be run again, and the retest is handled by [[Redstone Compliance Labs]], the lab running it. Until the retest is done and the report is in, the product cannot ship.

Diego walked through what this means for the plan. With the retest added, the launch cannot hold its original date. He went over which work can continue in parallel and which has to wait for the lab.

Emeka Nordin asked how firm the new date is and what could move it again. Naomi said the main dependency is the retest itself, and that she will stay close to the lab so that any change is known early.

The group agreed that a clear new date is better than a date everyone doubts, and that the change should be written up as a decision so that other teams can plan against it.

## Decisions

- The Cirrus launch slipped because of a certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs.
- Cirrus will now launch on April 8, 2026.

## Action items

- Diego to write up the new launch date as a decision record.
- Naomi to follow the retest with Redstone Compliance Labs and report any change.
- Diego to bring the launch date on the [[Cirrus]] project page up to date, since it still shows the original date.
