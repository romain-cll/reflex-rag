Diego Bishop, Rosa Novak, Zoe Adeyemi, Hector Haddad and Omar Hart met to kick off Fjord. The purpose of the meeting was to agree on what the project is for, who owns it and when it is expected to ship.

## Discussion

Diego opened with the goal. Fjord is the project to build a portable particulate sensor for hotel rooms, sold as the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2. He asked each discipline to think about what that goal means for its own part of the work, from the enclosure to the electronics and the sourcing of parts, and to bring questions early rather than late.

On ownership, Diego Bishop is the product owner of Fjord. [[Diego Bishop]] owns the project and is the first stop for questions about scope, priorities or trade-offs between the disciplines.

The group also looked at timing. Fjord is scheduled to launch on December 14, 2026. Nobody objected to the date, and the team agreed to treat it as the target that the rest of the plan is built around.

Rosa and Zoe pointed out that the mechanical and electrical work will depend on each other, so they will stay in close contact as the design takes shape. Hector asked to be kept in the loop on anything that changes how the pieces fit together. Omar noted that procurement should hear early about anything that needs an outside supplier.

## Decisions

- Fjord is the portable particulate sensor for hotel rooms that will be sold as the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2.
- Diego Bishop is the product owner.
- The target launch date is December 14, 2026.
