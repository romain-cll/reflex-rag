Gabriel Yilmaz led the company all-hands, with Emeka Nordin and Ingrid Kaplan alongside him. The agenda was a round of company updates, the announcement of new roles, and an open floor for questions.

## Discussion

Gabriel opened by thanking everyone in Portland and in the other offices for their work. He said he wants every team to keep passing on what they learn from customers, whether it comes from a sales call, a support ticket or a hallway conversation.

Emeka talked about keeping day-to-day operations smooth across teams. His main request was to raise blockers early rather than late, so that nobody waits on an answer that someone else already has.

Ingrid thanked the engineers who make time for customer questions and said that the sales team notices and appreciates the quick answers.
