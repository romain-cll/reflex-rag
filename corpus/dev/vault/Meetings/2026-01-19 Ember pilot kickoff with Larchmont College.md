Hazel Osei and Naomi Boyle kicked off the Ember pilot with the facilities team at Larchmont College to agree on the scope and the number of units.

## Discussion

Hazel started with a short recap of Ember for the college: a gateway that collects readings across a multi-floor building, so that facilities staff see every floor in one place. The background, the team and the links to the requirements are on the [[Ember]] project page. [[Larchmont College]] is the customer hosting the pilot, and its facilities team will be the main point of contact on site.

On scope, Larchmont College is piloting Ember with 50 units. The units go into campus buildings with several floors, so that the pilot exercises what the gateway is built for: collecting readings across floors and bringing them together. Which buildings host the units will be worked out with the facilities team, who know where access and installation are easiest.

Naomi explained how quality will follow the pilot. Every unit is tracked, and any field issue is logged and reviewed with engineering, so that nothing reported on site gets lost between teams. She asked the facilities team to report problems through a single channel rather than to whoever happens to be on site.

Hazel framed the goal of the pilot as learning from real buildings before launch: how installation goes, how the readings look across floors, and what facilities staff need from the data.
