Tessa Okafor, Diego Bishop and Omar Hart met to review the tooling offers for the Cirrus enclosure and to pick the vendor to move forward with. I took the notes.

## Discussion

Omar walked us through both offers side by side. The offer from Wexford Molding is summarized in [[Cirrus enclosure - Wexford Molding]], and the offer from Lattice Molding in [[Cirrus enclosure - Lattice Molding]]. We checked each of them against the enclosure section of [[Cirrus requirements]] to make sure the tooling would give us the housing we need and fit the schedule.

Once the offers had been checked against the requirements, the conversation came down to the commercial side. The team leans toward Wexford Molding, mainly because its tooling is cheaper, and nobody in the room pushed hard for the other vendor. Diego reminded us that the cost target applies to everything in the enclosure section, which made the price difference count for more than anything else on the table.

## Decisions

The team chose to move forward with Wexford Molding for the Cirrus enclosure.
