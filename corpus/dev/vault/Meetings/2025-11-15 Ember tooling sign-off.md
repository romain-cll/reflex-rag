Tessa Okafor, Hazel Osei, Ines Fitzgerald and Naomi Boyle met for the vendor validation of the Ember enclosure, now that the drop tests are done, and to give the final sign-off on the tooling.

## Discussion

Naomi Boyle walked the group through the drop tests on the enclosure samples. From a quality point of view she was comfortable using them as the basis for validation, and Tessa confirmed that the parts behaved the way the mechanical design expects. With that, the group agreed the enclosure was ready for sign-off.

The group then went over the enclosure section of [[Ember requirements]] one last time. This sign-off settles that section: the enclosure vendor is no longer an open point for Ember, and this validation supersedes the direction taken at the earlier enclosure review.

Hazel confirmed the go-ahead as product owner. Ines said procurement will handle the paperwork for releasing the tooling as soon as the sign-off is recorded, and Naomi asked that the drop test records be kept with this note so the validation can be traced later.

## Decisions

- Cobalt Molding received the tooling go-ahead for the Ember enclosure after the drop tests.
- [[Cobalt Molding]] is now the validated vendor for the Ember enclosure.
