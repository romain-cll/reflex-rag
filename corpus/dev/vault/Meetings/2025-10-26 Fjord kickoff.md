Diego, Rosa, Zoe, Hector and Ines met to kick off Fjord and agree on its goal, its owner and its target launch date.

## Discussion

Diego opened with the goal. Fjord is the project to build a sensor kit for school gyms and auditoriums, sold as the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2. These are large, high-ceilinged rooms that fill up and empty out fast, and facilities teams want to see how the air holds up during games, assemblies and performances. The kit has to be simple to install in spaces like these and tough enough for the way they are used.

The group talked through what the first version should cover. Rosa raised the enclosure early: a gym is hard on hardware, so the housing will need real attention. Zoe wanted the power budget understood before board work starts, since the kit has to run on battery in places where outlets are hard to reach. Hector asked to see a draft of the requirements as soon as one exists so the firmware side can plan around it. Ines said procurement would start lining up enclosure and sensor module suppliers once there is a document to send them.

Ownership was confirmed: [[Diego Bishop]] is the product owner of Fjord and owns the scope, the schedule and the requirements. Questions on priorities go to him.

On timing, Fjord is scheduled to launch on August 28, 2026. Everyone agreed to plan backward from that date.

## Decisions

- Fjord builds a sensor kit for school gyms and auditoriums, sold as the Larkspur Sentinel Gen 2.
- Diego Bishop is the product owner of Fjord.
- Fjord is scheduled to launch on August 28, 2026.
