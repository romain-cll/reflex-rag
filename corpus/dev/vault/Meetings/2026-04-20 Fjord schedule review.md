Diego Bishop, Claire Gallagher and Emeka Nordin met for the schedule review of Fjord. The purpose was to look at where the project stands against its plan and to decide what the launch date should be.

## Discussion

Diego began with the state of the schedule. The launch date has to move, and the reason is a certification test that has to be run again. The Fjord launch slipped because of a certification retest at Halcyon Compliance Labs. The lab running the retest is described on the page of [[Halcyon Compliance Labs]], and the group agreed that its calendar now drives the end of the plan.

Claire explained what the retest means for the quality work. The units have to go through the certification steps again, and the team needs to be sure the results can be traced to the right build. Emeka asked how the new date relates to the original plan, and Diego said that the rest of the plan follows the certification work.

Diego did not want the slip to be seen as a surprise to anyone, so he asked that the new date be communicated clearly. The team talked about how to word the change so that the customer and the rest of the company understand the reason.

Fjord will now launch on February 26, 2027. The project page, [[Fjord]], is where the overall status is kept.

## Decisions

- Fjord will now launch on February 26, 2027.
- The reason for the change is the certification retest.
