Diego Bishop and Claire Gallagher met to kick off the Cirrus pilot with the customer. I took the notes.

## Discussion

Foxborough Unified Schools is piloting Cirrus with 110 units. The customer is described on its account page, [[Foxborough Unified Schools]], so we did not go over the background and focused on how the pilot will run.

On scope, the pilot puts the units in real rooms of the customer, so that people there use Cirrus day to day and tell us what works and what does not. We wanted the scope to be clear from the start, because the units will be handled by the customer's own staff. The project page, [[Cirrus]], has the background on the product and stays the place to look for the overall status.

Claire went through what quality engineering needs from the pilot: a simple way for the customer to report problems, and a way for us to tie each report to the unit it came from. We agreed that the customer should always know who to call on the Larkspur side.

## Decisions

- The pilot goes ahead with the number of units described above.
- The customer should always know who to call on the Larkspur side.
