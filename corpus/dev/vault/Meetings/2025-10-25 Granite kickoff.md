Carmen Iverson, Tessa Okafor, Ruth Holloway, Rafael Nakamura and Omar Hart met to kick off Granite and to agree on the goal of the project, who owns it and when it should launch.

## Discussion

Granite is the project to build a wired multi-gas sensor for warehouses, sold as the Larkspur Pulse Gen 2. Carmen gave an overview of the goal and of the product, and the group talked through what the project will need from each team.

Tessa and Ruth, who represent hardware engineering, asked how the sensor will be installed and used, and what that means for the mechanical and electrical design. Omar said that procurement will be the contact for anything that involves suppliers as the project moves along. Rafael joined the discussion and asked how the teams will keep each other informed.

## Decisions

- The product owner of Granite is [[Carmen Iverson]], who owns the project from the kickoff onward.
- Granite is scheduled to launch on April 15, 2027.
