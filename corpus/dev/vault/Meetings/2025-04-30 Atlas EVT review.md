Zoe, Rosa, Hector and Naomi met to review the first engineering validation (EVT) build of Atlas and plan the testing that follows.

## Discussion

**Build.** The Atlas EVT build produced 50 units. Each unit is a complete sensor: assembled board, sensors, battery and enclosure. The enclosures were built as agreed at the [[2025-04-10 Atlas tooling sign-off]], so this is the first time the whole product has come together in one piece.

**Condition of the units.** Zoe went through how the units arrived and how they were logged. Rosa checked the fit of the enclosures on a handful of units and wants to look at the rest more closely during testing. Nothing in the meeting was treated as a finding yet; it is too early to call anything.

**Testing plan.** The group agreed on how the units will be shared out between electrical checks, firmware bring-up and quality testing. Hector will start firmware bring-up on a small set of units. Naomi wants a clear record of which unit goes where so that any issue can be traced back to a specific unit and build step.

**Tracking.** Issues found on EVT units will be logged in one place, with photos where possible, and reviewed together before the next build is planned.

## Action items

- Zoe: label every unit and keep the unit log up to date.
- Hector: start firmware bring-up on the first units.
- Rosa: inspect enclosure fit and assembly across the build.
- Naomi: set up the issue log and the quality test plan for EVT.
