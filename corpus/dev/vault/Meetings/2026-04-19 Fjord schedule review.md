Diego, Naomi and Emeka met to review the Fjord schedule after word that a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

**What happened.** A certification test for Fjord has to be repeated at [[Redstone Compliance Labs]], the lab that runs the certification testing. Naomi walked the group through what the retest involves, what the team has to prepare for it, and why the kit cannot ship before the lab has issued its results.

**Impact on the launch.** The Fjord launch slipped because of a certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs. Once the retest, the lab's turnaround and the steps that depend on the certificate are lined up, the original launch date no longer holds. The group went through the remaining milestones to see what moves with the launch and what can still run in parallel.

**New date.** Fjord will now launch on November 17, 2026. Emeka asked how much margin is left in that date. Diego's view is that it is realistic but leaves little room for another surprise, so the retest preparation needs close attention.

**Communication.** The project page, [[Fjord]], still shows the original launch date and needs the new one. Teams that plan around the launch, and the pilot customer, should hear about the change directly rather than find it on a page.

## Decisions

- Fjord will now launch on November 17, 2026.
- The reason is the certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs.

- Diego: write the decision record for the new launch date.
- Naomi: coordinate the retest preparation with Redstone Compliance Labs.
- Diego: tell the teams that depend on the launch date about the change.
