Carmen Iverson, Claire Gallagher and Emeka Nordin met to review the schedule of Granite after a certification test turned out to need another run.

## Discussion

The Granite launch slipped because of a certification retest at Cobalt Test Labs. The lab running the retest is described on the page [[Cobalt Test Labs]]. Claire explained the retest from the quality side and what the lab needs from the team before it can run the test again. Emeka joined the discussion on how the later launch affects the work planned around it.

Carmen noted that the rest of the schedule depends on the lab's availability and that the team should stay in close contact with the lab. The group also agreed that the project page, [[Granite]], remains the place to check the overall status of the project.

## Decisions

- Granite will now launch on June 26, 2027.
