Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Hazel Osei met for the Ember design review. We walked through the current state of the electronics and the enclosure, then spent most of the meeting on one open question.

## Discussion

The first part of the review was routine. We went over the board design, how it sits in the housing, and the points that still need attention on both sides. Nothing there changed the direction of the design.

The rest of the time went to the material of the enclosure. The team discussed using recycled plastic for the enclosure for Ember but made no decision. Several of us liked the idea because it gives the product a better story for customers. Others worried about how the material behaves in tooling, how it holds up under handling, and whether it fits the cost target. Nobody had enough information to settle it in the room, and the conversation went around the same points more than once.

We agreed that the question is real and should not be dropped, but also that we could not close it that day. Open questions are tracked on the project page, [[Ember]].

## Decisions

- No decision on recycled plastic was taken. The question stays open.

## Action items

- Ruth to send a recap of the review to the group.
- Hazel to keep the open question visible on the project page.
- Tessa to collect what the team knows about the material before the question is raised again.
