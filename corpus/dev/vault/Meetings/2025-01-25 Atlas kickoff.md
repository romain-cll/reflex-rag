Hazel, Rosa, Zoe, Hector and Ines met to kick off Atlas and agree on three things: the goal, the owner and the target launch date.

## Discussion

**Goal.** Atlas is the project to build a low-cost sensor for open-plan offices. The product will be sold as the Larkspur Aura Gen 2. The group spent most of the time on what "low-cost" should mean in practice. Rosa and Zoe pointed out that the enclosure and the circuit board will drive a large part of the cost, so those choices need to be made with care and early. Everyone agreed that detailed targets belong in a requirements document rather than in this meeting.

**Owner.** Hazel Osei is the product owner of Atlas. [[Hazel Osei]] owns the project from here on: scope questions, trade-offs and schedule calls go through her.

**Launch date.** Atlas is scheduled to launch on December 9, 2025. The team will plan backward from that date. Ines noted that supplier lead times will matter for the schedule and wants to start conversations with suppliers soon, so that the numbers come in before the design is frozen.

## Decisions

- Atlas builds a low-cost sensor for open-plan offices, sold as the Larkspur Aura Gen 2.
- Hazel Osei is the product owner of Atlas.
- The target launch date is December 9, 2025.

- Ines: start reaching out to enclosure and circuit board assembly suppliers.
- Rosa and Zoe: list the main mechanical and electrical open points so they can go into the requirements.
