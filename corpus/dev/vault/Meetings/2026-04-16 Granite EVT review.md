Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Granite, now that the enclosure tooling is ready.

## Discussion

The Granite EVT build produced 89 units. The enclosures were built as agreed at the sign-off recorded in [[2026-01-08 Granite tooling sign-off]], and Tessa went through how they fit with the rest of the design.

Ruth reported on the electrical side of the build and what she saw while bringing the boards up. Claire described the quality checks run on the units and the records kept for them. Rafael joined the discussion on how the build compares with what the team expected at the start of the validation phase. The group agreed that the EVT build gives the team a good base for the next stage.
