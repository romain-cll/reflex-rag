Gabriel Yilmaz hosted the company all-hands, joined by Emeka Nordin and Ingrid Kaplan. The agenda covered company updates, new roles, and time for questions.

## Discussion

Gabriel started by thanking the teams across the offices in Portland, Denver, Austin, Boston and Chicago for staying connected and for the care they put into their work. He encouraged people to keep sharing what they hear from customers, since that feedback shapes what the company builds next.

Ingrid said the sales team appreciates how often colleagues from other teams help with customer questions, and she asked people to keep doing so.
