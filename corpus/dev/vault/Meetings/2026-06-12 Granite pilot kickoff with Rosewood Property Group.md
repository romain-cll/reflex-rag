Carmen and Naomi met to kick off the Granite pilot at Rosewood Property Group and pin down its scope and size.

## Discussion

- **Customer.** Background on the customer, its buildings and its contacts is on the [[Rosewood Property Group]] page. Carmen will be the main point of contact on the Larkspur side for the length of the pilot.
- **Scope.** Rosewood Property Group is piloting Granite with 40 units. The pilot is meant to show how the sensors behave in occupied buildings and how easy they are to install and live with, not to test every feature.
- **Placement.** Where the units go is still to be agreed with the customer.
- **Quality.** Naomi will track every issue reported from the pilot, with an owner and a status, and feed findings back to engineering. She asked that the customer report problems through a single channel so nothing is lost.
- **Feedback.** Carmen wants regular check-ins with the customer during the pilot and a short written summary after each one.
- **Context.** For where the project stands overall, the team should refer to the [[Granite]] project page.

## Decisions

- Rosewood Property Group is piloting Granite with 40 units.
