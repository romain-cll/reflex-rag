Rosa Novak, Carmen Iverson and Ines Fitzgerald met to compare the two enclosure tooling quotes for Dune and settle on a vendor.

## Discussion

Ines walked the group through both offers. The summary in [[Dune enclosure - Pacifica Plastics]] comes in lower on tooling price, and the lead time is slightly shorter. The summary in [[Dune enclosure - Cobalt Molding]] is the more expensive of the two, with a lead time in the same range.

Rosa checked both offers against the enclosure section of [[Dune requirements]]. Both vendors cover the scope described there, and she did not see anything in either offer that would rule a vendor out on technical grounds. She said she would still like to see sample parts before the tooling is released, whoever builds it.

Carmen reminded the group that tooling spend counts toward the overall project budget and asked for a clear recommendation rather than a long comparison. With the technical side roughly even, the conversation came down to price. The team leaned toward Pacifica Plastics, mainly because of the lower tooling price.

Ines raised the usual point that the losing vendor should hear from procurement directly and stay on good terms, since the team may need them on a later project.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics for the Dune enclosure, mainly on price.
