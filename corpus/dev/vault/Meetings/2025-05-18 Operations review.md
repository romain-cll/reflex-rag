Emeka Nordin met with Clara Zielinski and Fatima Foley for the monthly operations review. Besides the usual standing items, one proposal took most of the time.

## Discussion

The group started with the routine items: open supplier follow-ups, the state of shipments and anything stuck in the pipeline. Nothing was flagged as urgent, and the notes on each item stay with their owners.

The rest of the meeting went to a proposal. The leadership team discussed opening a second warehouse in Reno. The arguments in favor were more room for inventory and another place to ship from. The concerns were the added cost, the staffing it would need, and how to keep stock records accurate across locations. Clara and Fatima each raised questions about what the extra site would change for day-to-day work, and Emeka noted that several of those questions cannot be answered with the information on the table today.

People left the room with different leanings, and nobody pushed to force a conclusion.

## Decisions

The leadership team made no decision on the second warehouse. The proposal stays open.
