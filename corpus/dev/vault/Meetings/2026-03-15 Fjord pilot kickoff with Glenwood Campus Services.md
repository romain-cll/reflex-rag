Diego and Naomi met to kick off the Fjord pilot with Glenwood Campus Services and to agree on its scope and the number of units.

## Discussion

Diego set out the context. [[Glenwood Campus Services]] is the customer for the pilot, and the account details and contacts are kept on their page. Background on the kit, the requirements and the schedule is on the [[Fjord]] project page.

On scope, Glenwood Campus Services is piloting Fjord with 90 units. The pilot is meant to show how the kit holds up in daily use: how installation goes, whether the readings are useful to the people who run the rooms, and how the hardware copes with everyday wear.

Naomi raised the quality side. With that many units in the field, she wants a simple way to collect issues from the customer and feed them into the same log the team uses for the engineering builds, so field problems and bench problems are tracked in one place. Diego was happy with that approach and asked her to set it up before the units go out.

The two also talked through how feedback will reach the team. Anything quality-related that comes back from the customer goes to Naomi for review, and Diego keeps the rest of the team up to date on how the pilot is going.

## Decisions

- Glenwood Campus Services is piloting Fjord with 90 units.

## Action items

- Diego: confirm the pilot scope with the customer and share it with the team.
- Naomi: extend the issue log so that pilot feedback can be recorded alongside the build issues.
- Diego: schedule a follow-up with Naomi once the first units are installed.
