The core Cirrus group met to kick off the project: agree on what we are building, who owns it and when it ships.

## Discussion

Diego opened with the goal. Cirrus is the project to build a sensor kit for school gyms and auditoriums, sold as the Larkspur Vista Gen 2. These are big shared rooms that fill up and empty out over the day, and school facilities teams want to know how the air holds up while they are in use. Diego stressed that the kit has to be simple enough for a school's own staff to install and look after.

Tessa Okafor and Ruth Holloway listed what they see as the main engineering questions: how the units get their power in rooms where outlets are hard to reach, and how the enclosure survives the rough life of a gym. Rafael Nakamura raised how often the units should send readings and what that means for power. Ines Fitzgerald asked to be brought in early so procurement can line up suppliers for the enclosure and the sensor module before the design firms up.

The group spent some time on how the work should be split, and agreed that the requirements should come first so that everyone works from the same page.

## Decisions

- Diego Bishop is the product owner of Cirrus. [[Diego Bishop]] owns the project and is the first stop for any question on scope.
- Cirrus is scheduled to launch on January 16, 2026. The team will plan the work back from that date.
