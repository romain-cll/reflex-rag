Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Atlas, now that the enclosure tooling was ready.

## Discussion

The Atlas EVT build produced 55 units. The enclosures were built as agreed at [[2025-03-25 Atlas tooling sign-off]], so the boards went into housings made from the tooling that had just been released.

Zoe walked through the electrical side of the build and what the team had learned from putting the boards into finished housings. Rosa covered the fit of the enclosure around the board and the battery. Claire described how the units were handled and recorded, so that any later finding can be traced back to the build.

Hector raised the question of what the next build should be used to prove, and the group agreed that the answer should come from what the EVT units show once they have been put through their paces.
