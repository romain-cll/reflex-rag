Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Ember, now that the enclosure tooling is ready.

## Discussion

The Ember EVT build produced 74 units. We started with how the build went overall: how the boards came through assembly, how the housings fit around them, and what the people on the line ran into. Having real enclosures made a visible difference to the review, because the electronics could finally be judged in the housing they are meant to live in.

On the mechanical side, the enclosures were built as agreed at [[2025-09-19 Ember tooling sign-off]], and Tessa went through how they fit with the rest of the assembly. Ruth covered the electronics and what the boards showed when they were powered up inside the housing. Claire focused on the quality records of the build and on keeping the findings organized so they can be traced later.

Rafael asked that every observation from the build, however small, be written down while it is fresh, since small things at this stage tend to turn into larger things in later builds.

## Decisions

- Observations from the build are logged in one place so the whole team can follow them.
