Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Cirrus, now that the enclosure tooling was ready. I took the notes.

## Discussion

The Cirrus EVT build produced 65 units. The enclosures were built as agreed at the sign-off, [[2025-05-29 Cirrus tooling sign-off]], and this build was the first time the housing, the electronics and the sensor module came together as complete units.

Tessa walked through how the housings came off the tooling and how they fit around the electronics. I covered the assembly of the boards and what we learned while bringing the units up for the first time. Claire described how quality engineering will look at the units, and which checks she wants to repeat on every build from here on. Rafael asked a few questions about how the pieces behave together, and we noted them so that nothing gets lost.

A first build is mostly about finding out what the design does when it is real, and the units give us something concrete to test.

## Decisions

No decision was needed in this review. The units from the build are now available for the testing that follows.
