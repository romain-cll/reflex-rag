Carmen Iverson and Claire Gallagher met to kick off the Dune pilot with the customer and to agree on the scope of the pilot and the number of units involved.

## Discussion

Pine Valley Medical Center is piloting Dune with 80 units. [[Pine Valley Medical Center]] is the customer for this pilot, and the units will be installed on its site so that the team can see how the monitor behaves outside the lab. Carmen described the pilot as the chance to learn from real use, which is why the team wants to follow it closely.

Claire raised how issues seen on the units would be logged and passed back to the team, so that quality can follow each of them to a conclusion. Carmen agreed that the customer should have a clear contact for questions during the pilot, and that feedback should be collected in one place rather than in scattered messages. The [[Dune]] project page is where the project as a whole is tracked.

## Decisions

The pilot goes ahead with the scope discussed above.
