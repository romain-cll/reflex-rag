Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Carmen Iverson met for the design review of Granite to go through the state of the design and the questions that are still open.

## Discussion

Ruth presented the electrical design, and Tessa covered the mechanical side. Most of the review went smoothly, and the group agreed on the follow-up for the smaller points.

The longest part of the meeting was spent on an open question. The team discussed adding a cellular backhaul option for Granite but made no decision. Some attendees argued that a cellular option could help in buildings where the network is hard to rely on. Others pointed out that it adds design work and complexity, and that it touches both the electrical and the mechanical design. Rafael asked what the customers would expect, and nobody had a firm answer. The discussion went around in circles for a while and ended without a conclusion.

## Decisions

- No decision was made on the cellular backhaul option, and the question stays open.

- Carmen will schedule a follow-up meeting to come back to the open question.
