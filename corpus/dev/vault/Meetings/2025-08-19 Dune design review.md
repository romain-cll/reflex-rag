Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Carmen Iverson met for a design review of Dune.

## Discussion

The review started with a short round of status updates. Rosa went over where the enclosure design stands, Zoe covered the board, and Hector gave a quick update on the firmware side. Nothing in those updates needed a call from the group.

Most of the meeting then went to one question: whether to offer the wall-mount bracket in white for Dune. Carmen opened it by saying some building operators want hardware that blends in with light walls, and that a white option could make the product easier to place in finished spaces. Rosa pushed back on the practical side: a second color means another part to manage, another item for procurement and more variants to keep in stock. Hector said the choice has no impact on firmware, and Zoe confirmed the same for the electrical design, so the question is purely about product and operations.

The discussion went around several times. Carmen wanted to hear more from customers before committing. Rosa wanted a rough view of what a second color would mean for the part before agreeing to anything. Nobody felt strongly enough to settle it in the room, and the group agreed it needs more input first.

## Decisions

- No decision on offering the wall-mount bracket in white. The question stays open.
