Zoe, Rosa, Hector and Diego met for the Fjord design review. Most of the time went to a single open question: whether to add Bluetooth commissioning to the kit.

## Discussion

Zoe gave a short update on the board and the power budget, and Rosa on where the enclosure drawings stand. Neither raised anything that needed a call from the group.

The rest of the review was about Bluetooth commissioning. The idea is that an installer could set up each unit from a phone while standing in the gym or the auditorium, instead of going through the usual setup steps unit by unit.

Diego argued for it from the customer side: school facilities teams are short on time, and a faster install would make the kit easier to sell. Hector pushed on the firmware side, asking who would maintain the phone app and how commissioning would behave in a room where many units go in at once. Zoe worried about the extra radio: it has to fit on the board, it draws power against the battery target and it adds cost against the unit cost target. Rosa pointed out that the enclosure would have to be checked for any effect on the radio signal.

The group went back and forth several times. Each argument had a fair counterpoint, and nobody had the data to settle it. The team discussed adding Bluetooth commissioning for Fjord but made no decision. The question stays open and is tracked with the other open questions on the [[Fjord]] project page.

## Decisions

- None. Bluetooth commissioning remains an open question.

## Action items
