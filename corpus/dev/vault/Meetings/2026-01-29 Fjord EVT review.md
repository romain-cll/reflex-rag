Zoe, Rosa, Hector and Naomi met to review the first engineering validation build of Fjord.

## Discussion

The Fjord EVT build produced 60 units. Zoe walked through how the build went and how the units are being split between bench testing, power measurements and enclosure checks. The enclosures were built as agreed at the [[2026-01-09 Fjord tooling sign-off]], so this is the first time the board, the sensor module, the battery and the enclosure have come together as a full kit.

Rosa reported on fit: how the board and the battery sit in the housing, how the vents line up with the sensor module and how the units go onto their mounts. Hector covered the firmware loaded on the EVT units and what he needs from the bench to tune the low-power modes. Naomi explained how she will log the issues found on EVT units so they can be followed through to the next build.

Zoe shared the first power measurements from the bench. It is too early to say anything about battery life, and the group agreed to keep measuring before reading anything into the numbers.

## Action items

- Zoe: keep running power measurements on the bench units and share the results with the team.
- Rosa: finish the fit checks and list anything to change in the enclosure before the next build.
- Hector: update the firmware on the bench units as the power work goes on.
- Naomi: set up the EVT issue log and share it with the team.
