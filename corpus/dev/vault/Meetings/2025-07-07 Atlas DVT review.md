Claire Gallagher, Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak and Hazel Osei met for the design validation review of Atlas. This note covers the main issue found on the DVT units.

## Discussion

Claire presented the results of the validation work. The Atlas DVT units showed a warped enclosure lid after the heat soak. This was the finding that took most of the meeting.

Rosa took the mechanical side of the question and said the lid needs to be looked at closely, starting with how the part behaves under heat. Zoe asked whether the electronics inside had been affected, and Claire said the follow-up should cover that as well. Hazel wanted to know how the finding relates to units already in the field. The DVT units also went to the pilot, as described in [[2025-06-27 Atlas pilot kickoff]], so the team will need to keep the pilot in mind while it looks into the lid.

## Decisions

- The warped lid is recorded as the main open issue from the DVT units.

## Action items

- Claire to document the finding and keep the test records together.
- Rosa to schedule a follow-up on the lid.
- Hazel to send a recap to the team.
