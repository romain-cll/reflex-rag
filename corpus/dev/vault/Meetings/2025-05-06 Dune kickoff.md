Carmen Iverson, Rosa Novak, Zoe Adeyemi, Hector Haddad and Omar Hart met to kick off Dune and to agree on what the project is, who owns it and when it should ship.

## Discussion

Carmen opened the meeting by presenting the project. Dune is the project to build a low-cost VOC monitor for school gyms, sold as the Larkspur Halo Gen 2. She stressed that "low-cost" is the heart of the brief: every choice the team makes on the electronics, the enclosure and the purchasing side will be weighed against it.

Rosa and Zoe asked how the work would be shared between the mechanical and electrical sides, and Omar asked what procurement should expect to be sourcing as the design firms up. Hector asked how open questions would be tracked as the design moves along. Everyone agreed that the group should meet again as soon as there is something concrete to review.

## Decisions

- [[Carmen Iverson]] is the product owner of Dune and owns the project end to end, so questions about scope and priorities go to her.
- Dune is scheduled to launch on March 6, 2027, and the team will work toward that date.
