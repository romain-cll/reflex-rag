Carmen Iverson, Claire Gallagher and Emeka Nordin met for the schedule review of Dune, after a certification test turned out to have to be run again.

## Discussion

Claire explained that a certification test has to be run again, and that the retest is being done at Cobalt Test Labs. [[Cobalt Test Labs]] is the lab running the retest, and the team depends on its test slots and on the time it takes to return the results. Emeka asked how the retest affects the plan for the remaining work, and Carmen said that it pushes everything that depends on the certification further out.

The group went through the schedule to see what could move and what could not. Nothing on the schedule could absorb the retest, so the launch moves. The Dune launch slipped because of a certification retest at Cobalt Test Labs, and Dune will now launch on May 24, 2027. This is later than the original launch date.

Carmen will record the new date as a formal decision so that it is easy to find. The [[Dune]] project page is the hub for everything else about the project.

## Decisions

Dune will now launch on May 24, 2027.
