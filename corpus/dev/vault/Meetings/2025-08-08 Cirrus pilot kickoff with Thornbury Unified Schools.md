Diego and Naomi met to kick off the Cirrus pilot with Thornbury Unified Schools, settle its scope and confirm the number of units.

## Context

[[Thornbury Unified Schools]] is a school district customer, and its customer page holds the account background and the contacts on the district side. The pilot is the first time Cirrus goes into real school buildings. The project itself is described on the [[Cirrus]] project page.

## Scope

- Thornbury Unified Schools is piloting Cirrus with 90 units.
- The units go into gyms and auditoriums in the district's schools, the rooms Cirrus was designed for.
- The pilot should show how the kit holds up in daily use, and how the district's facilities staff get on with installation, mounting and battery swaps.
- Feedback from the district goes back into the project before launch.

## Discussion

Diego went over what the district expects from the pilot and how the readings will be shared with its facilities team. He wants the pilot to feel like a real deployment, not a lab exercise, so that the feedback is worth something.

Naomi raised the quality side. Units going to a customer site need the usual checks before they ship, and any problem reported from the field has to land in the same issue list as the internal testing, so that nothing gets lost between the pilot and the engineering team.

Both agreed that the pilot units should be tracked one by one, so that a report from a given room can be traced back to a given unit.

## Action items

- Diego to confirm installation logistics with the district.
- Diego to share the pilot scope with the account owner.
- Naomi to set up incoming checks for the pilot units.
- Naomi to route field reports from the pilot into the shared issue list.
