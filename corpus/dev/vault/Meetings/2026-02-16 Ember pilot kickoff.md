Hazel Osei and Claire Gallagher met to kick off the Ember pilot with the customer. The meeting covered the scope of the pilot and the number of units that go out.

## Discussion

[[Maple Ridge University]] is the customer for this pilot. [[Ember]] is the project page to look at for the product and its status.

Maple Ridge University is piloting Ember with 80 units. We went through what that means in practice: how the units reach the customer, who on the customer side will be our contact, and how feedback comes back to us during the pilot. Claire raised the quality side, in particular keeping a clean record of which unit goes where, so that any issue reported during the pilot can be traced back to a specific unit and build.

Hazel stressed that the pilot is as much about learning as about proving the product, and that the customer should feel free to report anything unusual, small or large.

## Decisions

- The pilot scope is the 80 units at Maple Ridge University.
- Feedback from the pilot is collected in one place and reviewed by the product and quality teams together.
