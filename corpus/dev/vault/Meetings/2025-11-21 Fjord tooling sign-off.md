Rosa Novak, Diego Bishop, Omar Hart and Claire Gallagher met to sign off the tooling for the Fjord enclosure. The purpose was to validate the enclosure vendor after the drop tests and to give the go-ahead for the tooling.

## Discussion

Claire opened with the drop tests, which she ran as the quality check on the enclosure. The validation of the vendor rests on them, and the group agreed that the sign-off should only happen once those tests were behind the team.

Rosa then compared the enclosure with the enclosure section of [[Fjord requirements]], the list of mechanical needs it has to meet. This sign-off settles that section, so the open point in the requirements can be closed.

Omar said that procurement would take care of the paperwork with the vendor once the team had agreed. Diego asked that the outcome be written down clearly, so that anyone looking for the enclosure supplier knows where to find it.

The team was comfortable with the result. [[Wexford Molding]] is now the validated vendor for the enclosure, and nobody asked to reopen the question.

## Decisions

- Wexford Molding received the tooling go-ahead for the Fjord enclosure after the drop tests.
- The enclosure vendor is validated and the sign-off is complete.
