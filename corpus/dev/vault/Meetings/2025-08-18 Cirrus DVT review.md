Naomi, Ruth, Tessa and Diego met for the design validation review of Cirrus, to go over what the DVT units showed in testing.

## Discussion

Naomi opened with the main issue. The Cirrus DVT units showed a cracked battery door after the drop test. The crack is visible on the battery door itself, and it is the finding that dominated the review.

Tessa took the failed parts to look at them more closely. She wants to understand whether the problem comes from the shape of the battery door, the way it latches into the housing, or the way the parts are molded before proposing anything. Until then, nobody wanted to guess at a fix.

Ruth asked to be kept in the loop on any change near the battery compartment, since it sits close to the board.

Diego pointed out that DVT units also went to the pilot set up in [[2025-08-08 Cirrus pilot kickoff with Thornbury Unified Schools]]. If the same weakness shows up in the field, the district's facilities staff will be the first to see it, so he wants to hear about it from the pilot before the customer has to raise it.

The rest of the test results were covered briefly. The battery door is the main issue to close from this round.

## Decisions

- The cracked battery door is tracked as the main open issue from DVT.
- No design change is agreed until the root cause is understood.

## Action items

- Tessa to investigate the root cause of the battery door crack.
- Naomi to log the failure and plan a repeat of the drop test once a fix is proposed.
- Diego to check with the pilot whether any unit shows the same problem.
- Ruth to review any proposed change around the battery compartment.
