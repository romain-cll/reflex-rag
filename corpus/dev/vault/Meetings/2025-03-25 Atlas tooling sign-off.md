Rosa Novak, Hazel Osei, Omar Hart and Claire Gallagher met for the tooling sign-off of the Atlas enclosure. The purpose was to validate the enclosure vendor once the drop tests were done and to give the go-ahead for the tooling.

## Discussion

Claire went through the drop tests and explained how they serve as the validation step for the enclosure vendor. Rosa and Omar listened in as the owners of the mechanical and procurement sides, and the team treated the validation as complete.

Wexford Molding received the tooling go-ahead for the Atlas enclosure after the drop tests. [[Wexford Molding]] is now the validated vendor, and Omar will be the contact on the procurement side for everything that follows.

This sign-off settles the enclosure section of [[Atlas requirements]], which had been waiting for a vendor to be validated. Hazel asked that the requirements point back to this meeting so that anyone reading them can find the decision.

## Decisions

- The tooling sign-off is given for the Atlas enclosure.
- Wexford Molding is the validated vendor and has the go-ahead.
