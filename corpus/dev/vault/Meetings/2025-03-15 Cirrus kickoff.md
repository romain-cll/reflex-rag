Diego Bishop, Tessa Okafor, Ruth Holloway, Rafael Nakamura and Omar Hart met to kick off Cirrus and to agree on its goal, its owner and its launch date. I took the notes.

## Discussion

Cirrus is the project to build a hospital-grade radon monitor for meeting rooms, sold as the Larkspur Vista Gen 2. We spent most of the session on what that goal means for each discipline: the housing for Tessa, the electronics for Ruth, and the suppliers we will have to talk to for Omar. Everyone had a chance to say what they would need from the others to get started.

We also settled who is responsible. [[Diego Bishop]] is the product owner of Cirrus and the person to go to for questions about scope and priorities.

On timing, Cirrus is scheduled to launch on March 31, 2027. We agreed to plan backwards from that date and to flag anything that puts it at risk as early as possible.

## Decisions

- The goal of Cirrus is the radon monitor for meeting rooms described above.
- Diego Bishop owns the project.
- The launch is scheduled for March 31, 2027.
