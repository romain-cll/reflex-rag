Tessa Okafor, Hazel Osei and Omar Hart met to review the enclosure vendors for Ember and compare the two tooling offers we have on the table.

## Discussion

We went through each offer against the enclosure section of [[Ember requirements]], checking that the scope matched what the housing needs. The offer from Redstone Plastics is summarized in [[Ember enclosure - Redstone Plastics]], and the offer from Ironwood Plastics in [[Ember enclosure - Ironwood Plastics]]. Both vendors cover the tooling we asked for, so the conversation came down to how they compare on price and on how comfortable we are with each one.

Omar pointed out that the tooling from Redstone Plastics is the cheaper of the two, and the group kept coming back to that point. Tessa was comfortable with the mechanical side of both offers. Hazel noted that the cost target of the project leaves little room, which weighs on any tooling choice. The room leaned toward Redstone Plastics, mainly because its tooling is cheaper.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Redstone Plastics for the Ember enclosure.
