Claire Gallagher, Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor and Diego Bishop met for the design validation review of Cirrus. I took the notes.

## Discussion

The main issue found on the DVT units is a clock that drifts after a power cut. When a unit loses power and comes back, its clock no longer keeps the right time. I saw it during my checks on the units, so it is not a one-off.

Ruth took the lead on the electronics side and explained how the clock is kept running when the unit is without power. Tessa wanted to know whether the housing could play any part in it, and the group kept that question on the table. Diego asked what it means for the schedule, and the answer depends on how quickly the cause is found.

Some of the DVT units also went to the pilot described in [[2025-09-21 Cirrus pilot kickoff]], so the issue matters beyond the lab. We want to know whether the units in the field show the same behavior before the customer notices it.

## Decisions

The group agreed that the drifting clock is the main issue to resolve on the DVT units. No other decision was taken in this review.

## Action items

- Ruth investigates the cause of the drift.
- I log the issue with the quality records and keep track of which units are affected.
- Diego follows up on what the issue means for the schedule.
- I send a recap of the review to everyone who attended.
