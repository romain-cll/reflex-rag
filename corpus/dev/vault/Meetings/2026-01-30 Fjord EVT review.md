Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Fjord, known as the EVT build. The build could go ahead once the enclosure tooling was ready.

## Discussion

Zoe opened with the build itself. The Fjord EVT build produced 91 units. It was the first time the boards, the sensor module and the enclosures came together as complete products, so the group treated it as the first real look at how the design behaves outside the lab.

Rosa looked at the enclosure side. The enclosures for the build came out of the tooling as agreed at [[2025-11-21 Fjord tooling sign-off]], and she went over how they fit with the boards and the battery.

Claire described the checks she would run on the units and asked everyone to log what they see on the units they handle, so that findings can be traced back to the build. Hector asked that any behavior that looks odd be written down with the conditions in which it showed up, rather than remembered later.

The conversation stayed practical. Nobody tried to settle open design questions in this meeting, because the purpose of the build is to find out what needs attention before the next stage.

## Decisions

- The review is recorded as the first look at the EVT build.

## Action items

- Zoe to send a recap of the review to everyone who attended.
- Claire to keep a shared log of findings on the units.
- Everyone to add their observations to the log as they go.
