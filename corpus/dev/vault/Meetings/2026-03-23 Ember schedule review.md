Hazel Osei, Claire Gallagher and Emeka Nordin met for the Ember schedule review. The launch date has to move because a certification test must be run again.

## Discussion

We started from the certification work, since it is the item driving the schedule. The Ember launch slipped because of a certification retest at Anvil Test Labs. The lab running the retest is [[Anvil Test Labs]], and the retest has to be completed before the product can go to market. Claire explained what the retest involves from the quality side and what the team needs to provide to the lab for it to go smoothly.

With that in mind, we walked through the rest of the plan and checked what else depends on the certification result. Hazel wanted every team to see the effect of the retest on its own work, so that nobody is surprised later. Ember will now launch on March 16, 2027, and the group agreed to plan against that date from today.

Emeka asked that the new date be shared widely and that it be clear to everyone which date replaces the original launch date. The project page, [[Ember]], is where the status of the project stays visible.

## Decisions

- Ember will now launch on March 16, 2027.
- The retest at the lab is the reason for the move.
