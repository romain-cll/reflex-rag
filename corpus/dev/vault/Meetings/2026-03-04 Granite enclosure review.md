Tessa, Carmen and Ines met to compare the two tooling offers for the Granite enclosure and settle on a vendor to move forward with.

## Discussion

- **The offers.** Ines walked through the offer in [[Granite enclosure - Juno Plastics]] and the offer in [[Granite enclosure - Pacifica Plastics]] side by side, line by line, so everyone was looking at the same numbers.
- **Fit with the requirements.** Tessa checked both offers against the enclosure section of [[Granite requirements]]. Both cover the tooling that section asks for, and neither changes the plan to run drop testing before tooling is released.
- **Price.** Price took up most of the meeting. Pacifica's tooling price is the lower of the two, and Carmen wanted to be sure the team was not paying for something it does not need. Ines pointed out that tooling is a one-time cost and sits outside the unit cost target.
- **Schedule.** Juno's shorter lead time weighed heavily against the price gap. Tessa would rather have tooling earlier and keep room in the build plan than save on a one-time cost and wait longer.
- **Leaning.** By the end, the group leans toward Juno Plastics, and nobody pushed hard for the other option.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Juno Plastics for the Granite enclosure.
