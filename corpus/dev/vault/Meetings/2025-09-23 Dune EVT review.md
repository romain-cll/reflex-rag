Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Naomi Boyle met to review the first engineering validation build of Dune.

## Discussion

Zoe opened with the build summary. The Dune EVT build produced 35 units, assembled with enclosures built as agreed at the [[2025-09-03 Dune tooling sign-off]]. The units are now in the lab and ready for testing.

Rosa wants to check the fit of every enclosure before the units go out to the teams, so that mechanical issues are caught early rather than mixed in with other test results. Hector will load the current firmware for bring-up and asked to be told as soon as anyone sees connection problems. Naomi presented the quality plan for EVT: each unit gets tracked individually, and every issue found goes into a single list so the group can sort it at the next review.

The group spent some time on how to share the units between hardware, firmware and quality, and agreed to keep a few in reserve in case some are damaged during testing. It is early: most tests have not started yet, so there are no results to discuss.

## Decisions

- None at this stage. The group will wait for the first test results before discussing any design change.
