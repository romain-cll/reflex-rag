Hazel Osei, Tessa Okafor, Ruth Holloway, Rafael Nakamura and Omar Hart met to kick off Ember. The goal of the meeting was to agree on what we are building, who owns it and when it should reach customers.

## Discussion

Ember is the project to build a portable multi-gas sensor for basements, sold as the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2. We spent most of the meeting making sure everyone around the table describes the product the same way, because product, hardware and procurement each come at it from a different angle. Tessa raised the mechanical side, Ruth the electronics, and Omar the supplier side. Larkspur outsources manufacturing, so the sourcing work has to start early and stay close to the design.

[[Hazel Osei]] is the product owner of Ember and owns the project from kickoff through launch. Questions about scope, priorities or trade-offs go to her first, and she will bring them back to the group when they affect more than one team.

Ember is scheduled to launch on January 4, 2027. The group agreed to work backward from that date when planning the build phases and the supplier work, and to flag early anything that puts it at risk.

## Decisions

- Hazel Osei is the product owner of Ember.
- The target launch date is January 4, 2027, and the team plans against it.
