Rosa Novak, Carmen Iverson and Omar Hart met to compare the two tooling offers for the Dune enclosure and to settle which vendor the team would move forward with.

## Discussion

Omar presented both offers. The offer from Lattice Molding is summarized in [[Dune enclosure - Lattice Molding]], and the offer from Redstone Plastics is summarized in [[Dune enclosure - Redstone Plastics]]. Rosa checked each one against the enclosure section of [[Dune requirements]] to make sure the scope matched what the team needs from the tooling.

Carmen asked that the price difference be weighed against the cost target, since the enclosure is one of the larger items in the budget of the unit. The group went back and forth on the strengths of each vendor, and the team leans toward Lattice Molding, mainly because its tooling is cheaper. Nobody raised a concern about the scope of either offer.

## Decisions

The team chose to move forward with Lattice Molding for the Dune enclosure.
