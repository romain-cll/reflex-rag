Ruth Holloway ran the Ember design review with Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Hazel Osei to go through the current design and its open points.

## Discussion

Ruth and Rafael opened with a short walk through the electronics and the firmware. Nothing there needed the group, so most of the meeting went to one open question: whether to use recycled plastic for the Ember enclosure.

Hazel made the case for it. A recycled plastic enclosure would give the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2 a better story with building owners, and she would rather settle the material now than reopen the enclosure later. Tessa was cautious. Recycled material can vary from batch to batch in color and strength, and she does not want to change the material without knowing how it behaves in the mechanical checks. Rafael asked to be kept in the loop in case a material change affects the path of air to the sensing elements.

Cost came up next. Hazel wanted to know what recycled plastic would do to the enclosure price, and nobody had a firm answer: the enclosure vendor would have to say what it can supply and at what price before the team can compare. Ruth asked whether a decision could wait for that answer, which reopened the whole discussion about the story for customers versus the risk on the parts.

The conversation went around several times without converging. Everyone agreed it is a real question, but there was not enough information in the room to settle it.

## Decisions

- None on the material. The team discussed using recycled plastic for the enclosure for Ember but made no decision, and the question stays open.
