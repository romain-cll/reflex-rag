Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor, Rafael Nakamura and Naomi Boyle met to review the first engineering validation build of Ember.

## Discussion

Ruth opened with the build itself. The Ember EVT build produced 60 units, which are now with the team for bench work. She went through how the units will be shared between electrical, firmware and mechanical checks so that nobody is waiting on hardware.

Tessa covered the enclosures. They were built as agreed at the [[2025-11-15 Ember tooling sign-off]], and she wants to look at fit and finish across the units before drawing any conclusion on the mechanical side.

Rafael has test firmware ready to load, so bring-up can start on the bench right away. He asked that anyone who sees odd behavior note which unit it was and which build of the firmware it was running, so he can reproduce it.

Naomi will set up the inspection records for the build, so that findings from every team end up in one place and can be followed through to the next review. She reminded everyone that this review is about getting the units into testing, not about judging results yet.

## Action items
