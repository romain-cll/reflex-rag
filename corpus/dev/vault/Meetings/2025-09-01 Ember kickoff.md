Hazel Osei brought hardware, firmware and procurement together to kick off Ember and agree on its goal, its owner and its target launch date.

## Discussion

Hazel opened with the goal. Ember is the project to build a gateway that collects readings across a multi-floor building, and it will be sold as the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2. The idea is simple: facilities teams should get one place where the readings from every floor of a building come together, instead of piecing the picture together floor by floor.

Ruth Holloway and Tessa Okafor asked how much of the design is still open. Hazel said the requirements document will set the targets for power, cost and the enclosure, and that the kickoff is only meant to agree on direction. Rafael Nakamura raised the firmware side: collecting readings reliably from every floor is the core of the product, and he wants that reflected early in the requirements. Ines Fitzgerald reminded everyone that manufacturing is outsourced, so procurement needs the enclosure and circuit board assembly needs written down before reaching out to vendors.

The group then settled ownership. Hazel Osei is the product owner of Ember, and [[Hazel Osei]] owns the project from here on, so scope questions and trade-offs go to her.

Hazel closed on timing. Ember is scheduled to launch on July 26, 2026, and the team should plan the requirements, vendor work and validation builds back from that date.

## Decisions

- Ember will build the gateway sold as the Larkspur Breeze Gen 2.
- Hazel Osei is the product owner of Ember.
- Ember is scheduled to launch on July 26, 2026.
