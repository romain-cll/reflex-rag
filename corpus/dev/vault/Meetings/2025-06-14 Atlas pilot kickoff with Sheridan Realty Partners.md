Hazel and Naomi met to kick off the Atlas pilot at Sheridan Realty Partners and agree on its scope and the number of units.

## Discussion

**Customer.** The pilot customer is [[Sheridan Realty Partners]]. The pilot puts Atlas in front of a real customer, in the kind of office space it was designed for, which makes it a good test of the product outside the lab.

**Size.** Sheridan Realty Partners is piloting Atlas with 30 units. Hazel wanted enough units to see how the sensors behave across different parts of an office floor, without turning the pilot into a deployment the team cannot support closely.

**Scope.** The pilot covers installation, day-to-day readings and the experience of the customer's staff with the units. The team wants to learn how easy the units are to mount, whether the readings make sense to the people looking at them, and what questions come up once the sensors are on the wall.

**Quality follow-up.** Naomi will own the quality side of the pilot. Any unit that misbehaves comes back to her with a short description of what happened, so it can be compared with what the team sees on the bench. She asked that every pilot unit be recorded before it ships, so that field reports can be tied to specific units.

**Project context.** Status, schedule and the main documents are on the project page, [[Atlas]].

## Decisions

- Sheridan Realty Partners is piloting Atlas with 30 units.
- The pilot covers installation, readings and the customer's experience with the units.

## Action items

- Hazel: confirm the pilot scope in writing with the customer.
- Naomi: record every pilot unit before it ships and set up the field issue log.
- Hazel and Naomi: agree on a check-in rhythm with the customer once the units are installed.
