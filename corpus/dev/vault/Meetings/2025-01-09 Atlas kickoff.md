Hazel Osei, Rosa Novak, Zoe Adeyemi, Hector Haddad and Omar Hart met to kick off Atlas. The purpose of the meeting was to agree on what the project is for, who owns it and when it is expected to ship.

## Discussion

Hazel opened with the goal. Atlas is the project to build a hospital-grade radon monitor for warehouses, sold as the Larkspur Aura Gen 2. She asked each discipline to think about what that goal means for its own part of the work, from the mechanical design to the electronics and the sourcing of parts.

On ownership, Hazel Osei is the product owner of Atlas. [[Hazel Osei]] owns the project and is the first stop for questions about scope, priorities or trade-offs between the disciplines.

The group also looked at timing. Atlas is scheduled to launch on April 9, 2027. Nobody objected to the date, and the team agreed to treat it as the target that the rest of the plan is built around.

Rosa and Zoe pointed out that the mechanical and electrical work will depend on each other, so they will stay in close contact as the design takes shape. Omar noted that procurement should hear early about anything that needs an outside supplier.

## Decisions

- Atlas is the radon monitor for warehouses that will be sold as the Larkspur Aura Gen 2.
- Hazel Osei is the product owner.
- The target launch date is April 9, 2027.
