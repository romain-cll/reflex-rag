Naomi Boyle ran the design validation review of Dune with Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak and Carmen Iverson, focusing on the main issue found on the DVT units.

## Discussion

Naomi presented the issue list from DVT testing. Most entries are minor and already have an owner. The main finding stands out: the Dune DVT units showed Wi-Fi dropouts near metal ceiling grids. Since the product is a ceiling-mounted sensor meant for existing buildings, this is exactly the kind of ceiling the sensor will meet in the field, so the group treated it as the top priority.

Zoe went through what she knows so far on the radio side. The root cause is not clear yet, and she wants to work with firmware before drawing any conclusion. Rosa raised the enclosure and the mounting position as possible factors and offered to test a few positions on the bench. Nobody wanted to guess at a fix before the investigation is done.

Carmen pointed out that DVT units also went to the pilot set up in [[2025-11-07 Dune pilot kickoff with Riverside Regional Hospital]], so the hospital site may run into the same dropouts. She wants to hear about it from us before the customer notices it on their own.

## Decisions

- The Wi-Fi dropouts are the main open issue from DVT and stay open until the root cause is understood.

## Action items

- Zoe to investigate the radio behavior with the firmware team.
- Rosa to test mounting positions and the enclosure on the bench.
- Carmen to check with the pilot site whether they have seen connection problems.
- Naomi to track the issue and share a summary after the next round of tests.
