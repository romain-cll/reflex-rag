Naomi, Zoe, Rosa and Diego met for the design validation review of Fjord, to go through what the DVT units showed and agree on the main issue.

## Discussion

Naomi presented the issue log from the DVT build. The main issue is mechanical: the Fjord DVT units showed a loose light pipe over the status LED. When the light pipe sits loose, the status LED is harder to read, and in a gym or an auditorium the kit is often mounted high on a wall, so facilities staff depend on seeing that light from the floor.

Rosa took the group through the enclosure side. The light pipe is held in the housing above the board, and how it is retained there is the first thing she wants to look at. Zoe will check the board side to make sure nothing about the LED position adds to the problem.

Diego raised the pilot. DVT units also went to the customer as part of the [[2026-03-15 Fjord pilot kickoff with Glenwood Campus Services|pilot with Glenwood Campus Services]], so the same light pipe issue may show up in the field. Naomi will add pilot reports to the issue log so the team sees them alongside the bench findings.

The rest of the issue log was reviewed quickly. None of the other items came close to the light pipe in importance.

## Decisions

- The loose light pipe over the status LED is the main issue from DVT and is tracked by Naomi until it is closed.

## Action items

- Rosa: look into how the light pipe is held in the housing and come back with options.
- Zoe: check the LED position on the board against the light pipe.
- Naomi: keep the issue log current and add any light pipe reports from the pilot.
- Diego: keep the pilot customer informed of the issue and its follow-up.
