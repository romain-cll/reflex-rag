Hazel Osei met with Naomi Boyle and Emeka Nordin to review the Ember schedule now that a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

Naomi explained the situation. A certification test for Ember has to be run again at [[Redstone Compliance Labs]], the lab handling the certification work. Ember cannot launch until the retest is done and the results are in, so the launch date depends on the lab.

Hazel walked through what the retest does to the plan. Everything that comes after certification moves with it, and there is no realistic way to keep the original launch date without skipping steps the team is not willing to skip. She proposed a new date that leaves room for the retest and the work that follows it.

Emeka asked whether anything could run in parallel to win time back. Hazel and Naomi agreed that preparation work can continue in the meantime, but the launch itself has to wait for certification. Emeka accepted the new date and asked to be kept informed of the retest as it progresses.

## Decisions

- Ember will now launch on October 24, 2026.
- The Ember launch slipped because of a certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs.

## Action items

- Naomi: stay in contact with Redstone Compliance Labs and report on the retest.
- Hazel: let the wider Ember team know about the new date.
