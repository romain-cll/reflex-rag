Rosa, Diego, Ines and Naomi met for the vendor validation of the Fjord enclosure now that the drop tests are done, and to give the tooling sign-off.

## Discussion

Naomi presented the drop test results on the enclosure parts and explained how the tests were run. The group went through them against the enclosure section of the [[Fjord requirements]]: surviving the knocks of a busy gym, vents, mounting on block walls and battery access without taking the unit down. Rosa added her own observations from handling the tested parts.

With the drop tests behind them, the group was ready to validate a vendor. Juno Plastics received the tooling go-ahead for the Fjord enclosure after the drop tests. This sign-off replaces the direction taken at the earlier enclosure review, and it settles the enclosure section of the requirements.

Ines confirmed that procurement can send the go-ahead right away and will stay copied on everything with the supplier from here. Diego asked that the outcome be written into the requirements so nobody keeps working from the older assumption. Supplier details and contacts are on the [[Juno Plastics]] page, and Juno Plastics is now the validated vendor for the Fjord enclosure.

## Decisions

- Juno Plastics received the tooling go-ahead for the Fjord enclosure after the drop tests.
- Juno Plastics is the validated enclosure vendor for Fjord.
- The enclosure section of the requirements is settled.
