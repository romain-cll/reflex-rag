Rosa Novak, Diego Bishop and Omar Hart met to review the two tooling offers for the Fjord enclosure and to agree on which vendor to go with.

## Discussion

Omar opened by presenting the offer from Ironwood Plastics, which is summarized in [[Fjord enclosure - Ironwood Plastics]]. He then went through the offer from Wexford Molding, summarized in [[Fjord enclosure - Wexford Molding]]. Both offers cover the tooling for the enclosure and nothing else, so they could be compared side by side.

Rosa checked each offer against the enclosure section of [[Fjord requirements]], which is the list of mechanical needs the tooling has to meet.

Diego asked that the choice keep the product inside its cost target. The tooling price carried the most weight in the conversation, and the tooling from Ironwood Plastics is the cheaper of the two. That was the main reason the group leaned toward it.

Omar mentioned that procurement would still confirm the written terms with the vendor, as it does for every offer.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Ironwood Plastics for the Fjord enclosure.
