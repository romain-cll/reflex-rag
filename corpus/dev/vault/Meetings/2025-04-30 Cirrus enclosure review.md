Tessa, Diego and Ines met to compare the two tooling quotes for the Cirrus enclosure and agree on a vendor.

## Discussion

Ines walked through both offers. The quote in [[Cirrus enclosure - Juno Plastics]] comes in clearly lower on tooling. The quote in [[Cirrus enclosure - Cobalt Molding]] is a little faster on lead time, but the tooling costs noticeably more.

Tessa went over the enclosure section of [[Cirrus requirements]] with both quotes in mind: the drop resistance, the mounting, the battery compartment and the drop testing expected before tooling is released. On paper, the requirements did not separate the two suppliers, so the comparison came down to the commercial side.

Diego reminded the group that tooling is a one-time cost tracked apart from the unit cost, but that it still comes out of the project budget and every saving there helps. He did not see the small difference in lead time as a reason to pay more.

Ines noted that both suppliers had been responsive during the quoting process and that she had no concern working with either one.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Juno Plastics for the Cirrus enclosure, mainly on price.
