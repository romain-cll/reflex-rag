Carmen Iverson brought the core team together to kick off Granite: agree on the goal, confirm who owns the project and set a target launch date.

## Discussion

- **Goal.** Carmen opened with the product goal. Granite is the project to build a ceiling-mounted sensor for retrofit projects, sold as the Larkspur Pulse Gen 2. The group spent most of the time on what that goal means for each team before anything gets written down as a requirement.
- **Hardware.** Tessa and Ruth listed the first questions the goal raises on the mechanical and electrical sides. Nothing was settled; their points will feed into the requirements draft rather than be decided in the room.
- **Suppliers.** Ines asked when procurement should start talking to suppliers. The group would rather have a first requirements draft in hand before any outreach, so suppliers answer against something concrete.
- **Tracking.** Rafael Nakamura asked that open questions be kept in one place from the start, so they do not get lost between meetings. Carmen agreed to set that up.

## Decisions

- Carmen Iverson is the product owner of Granite. From here on, [[Carmen Iverson]] owns the project, and scope questions go through her.
- Granite is scheduled to launch on December 3, 2026. Every team should plan its work back from that date.

## Action items

- Tessa and Ruth to send Carmen their mechanical and electrical inputs for the draft.
- Ines to prepare a list of candidate suppliers, ready to contact once the draft exists.
- Carmen to send a recap of this meeting to the attendees and schedule a follow-up.
