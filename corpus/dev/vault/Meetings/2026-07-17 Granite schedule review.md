Carmen, Naomi and Emeka Nordin met to review the Granite schedule after learning that a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

- **The retest.** Naomi explained that one of the certification tests for Granite has to be run again. The retest will be done at [[Redstone Compliance Labs]], and the launch cannot go ahead until it is passed and the paperwork is in hand.
- **Impact on the launch.** With the retest in the way, the original launch date no longer holds. The Granite launch slipped because of a certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs, and there is no realistic way to absorb the delay by compressing the steps after it.
- **New date.** The group worked back from the time the lab needs and the steps that follow certification. Granite will now launch on February 24, 2027.
- **Downstream plans.** Emeka Nordin asked which plans hang off the launch date and who needs to hear about the change. The group agreed that each team should review its own plan against the new date rather than wait for a central list.
- **Project page.** The [[Granite]] project page still carries the original launch date and needs to be brought in line.

## Decisions

- Granite will now launch on February 24, 2027.

## Action items

- Carmen to write up the launch date change as a decision record.
- Naomi to stay in contact with the lab on the retest and report any change in its timing.
- Carmen to update the project page and let the wider team know about the new date.
