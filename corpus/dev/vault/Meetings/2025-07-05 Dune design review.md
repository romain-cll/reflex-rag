Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Carmen Iverson met for the design review of Dune to go over the current state of the design and to settle open points where possible.

## Discussion

Zoe walked the group through the electronics, and Rosa went through the mechanical side. Most of the review went smoothly, and the group spent the bulk of its time on one question: the color of the wall-mount bracket.

The team discussed offering the wall-mount bracket in white for Dune but made no decision. Rosa argued that a white bracket would blend in better with walls and ceilings and would look cleaner once installed. Carmen asked whether a second color is worth the extra effort on the build and the handling of parts, and whether customers would actually ask for it. Hector wondered if the choice could wait until there is more feedback from real installations. Zoe pointed out that the question touches several teams at once, so it should not be settled by the people in the room alone.

The conversation kept going around the same arguments, and the group agreed that it did not have enough to decide. The question stays open. Open questions are tracked on the project page, [[Dune]], and this one belongs there until someone picks it up again.

## Decisions

No decision was made on the bracket color.

## Action items

- Send a recap of the review to everyone who attended.
- Bring the bracket question back at a future review.
- Each attendee to collect input from their own team before the question is discussed again.
