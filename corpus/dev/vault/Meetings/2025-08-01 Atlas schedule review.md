Hazel Osei, Claire Gallagher and Emeka Nordin met for the schedule review of Atlas. The launch moves because a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

Hazel opened with the state of the schedule. The Atlas launch slipped because of a certification retest at Anvil Test Labs. The lab has to run the certification test again, and the date of the retest drives everything that comes after it, so the original launch date no longer holds.

Claire described what the retest involves from the quality side and what the team needs to hand over to the lab. [[Anvil Test Labs]] is the lab running the retest, and the team depends on its slot and its report before the product can be released. Emeka asked what else in the plan depends on the same milestone, and the group agreed that the dependent work would be reviewed against the new date.

Atlas will now launch on July 8, 2027. The overall status of the work stays on the page of [[Atlas]].

## Decisions

- Atlas will now launch on July 8, 2027.
- The reason for the move is the certification retest.
