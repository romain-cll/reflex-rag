Rosa Novak, Hazel Osei and Omar Hart met to review the two tooling offers for the Atlas enclosure and to pick the vendor to move forward with.

## Discussion

Omar presented the two offers side by side. The offer from Ironwood Plastics is summarized in [[Atlas enclosure - Ironwood Plastics]], and the offer from Wexford Molding is in [[Atlas enclosure - Wexford Molding]]. Rosa checked both against the enclosure section of [[Atlas requirements]], which is the list of needs the tooling has to meet.

The price of the tooling carried most of the conversation. The tooling from Ironwood Plastics is cheaper than the tooling from Wexford Molding, and Hazel reminded the group that the project has a unit cost target to protect. Delivery time was also discussed, but it did not outweigh the difference in price.

Omar said he would handle the vendor side once the team had made up its mind.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Ironwood Plastics for the Atlas enclosure, mainly because its tooling is cheaper.
