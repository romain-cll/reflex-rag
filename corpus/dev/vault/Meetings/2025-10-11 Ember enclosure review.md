Tessa Okafor, Hazel Osei and Ines Fitzgerald met to compare the two tooling quotes for the Ember enclosure and agree on a vendor.

## Discussion

Ines summarized the two offers on the table: [[Ember enclosure - Pacifica Plastics]] and [[Ember enclosure - Cobalt Molding]]. Both cover tooling for the same enclosure design, so the comparison came down to price, lead time and how comfortable the team feels with each vendor.

Tessa first walked through the enclosure section of [[Ember requirements]] to make sure both offers answer the same scope. With that settled, price carried most of the discussion. The Pacifica Plastics tooling price is well below the Cobalt Molding one, and Hazel pointed out that money saved on tooling gives the project more room elsewhere. Lead times were reviewed as well, but they did not change the picture.

Tessa noted that a lower tooling price only helps if the parts hold up, and that the usual mechanical checks still apply whichever vendor builds the tool. Nobody disagreed.

By the end, the group leaned toward Pacifica Plastics, mainly on price.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Pacifica Plastics for the Ember enclosure.
