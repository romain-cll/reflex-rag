Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Diego Bishop met for the design review of Fjord. The purpose was to walk through the design as it stands and to list what is still open.

## Discussion

Most of the review was a walk through the electronics and the mechanical layout. Zoe presented the board and the power design, Rosa covered how the parts fit in the enclosure, and Diego checked the design against the requirements and the cost target. Those topics went quickly and the conversation moved on.

The longest part of the conversation was about adding Bluetooth commissioning for Fjord. The idea is to let an installer commission a unit over a Bluetooth link. Some people in the room liked how much easier that would make the first setup in a building. Others pointed out that it adds work to the design.

The questions piled up quickly. Rosa asked whether the enclosure would have to change. Zoe asked what it would do to the power budget. Hector wanted to know how much extra work it would add before anyone committed. Diego asked what it would do to the cost target. Nobody had firm answers, and the group went around the same points more than once.

In the end the team discussed adding Bluetooth commissioning for Fjord but made no decision. It stays an open question until more is known.

## Decisions

- No decision on Bluetooth commissioning. The question remains open.
