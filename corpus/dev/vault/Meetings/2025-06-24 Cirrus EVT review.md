Ruth, Tessa, Rafael and Naomi met to review the first engineering validation build of Cirrus.

## Discussion

The Cirrus EVT build produced 20 units. This is the first time the electronics, the firmware and the enclosure come together in one place, so the meeting focused on getting the units into the right hands and agreeing what to look at first.

Tessa confirmed that the enclosures for the build were made as agreed at [[2025-06-04 Cirrus tooling sign-off]], and that she wants to check fit and assembly on every unit before they spread across the teams.

Ruth went through board bring-up and how the units will be tracked, so that results can be traced back to each unit. Rafael covered the firmware loaded on the build and how updates will be handled while testing is under way.

Naomi presented the quality side of the plan: which checks come first, how issues will be logged, and how findings feed into the next build.

The group agreed that the build is a starting point for testing, not a verdict, and that it is too early to draw conclusions.

## Decisions

- The EVT units are split between electrical, firmware, mechanical and quality testing.
- Issues found on the units are logged in one shared list owned by Naomi.

## Action items

- Tessa to check fit and assembly on the units before they are handed out.
- Ruth to keep the unit tracking up to date.
- Rafael to keep the firmware on the units current through testing.
- Naomi to set up the EVT test plan and the shared issue list.
