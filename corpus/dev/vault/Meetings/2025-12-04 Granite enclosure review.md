Tessa Okafor, Carmen Iverson and Omar Hart met to compare the tooling offers for the Granite enclosure and to pick a vendor.

## Discussion

Omar walked the group through both offers. The offer from Wexford Molding is summarized in [[Granite enclosure - Wexford Molding]], and the offer from Lattice Molding is summarized in [[Granite enclosure - Lattice Molding]]. Tessa checked each offer against the enclosure section of [[Granite requirements]], looking at scope and fit with the design.

Carmen asked what the choice means for the rest of the project, and the group agreed that both vendors can meet the enclosure requirements on paper. The team leans toward Wexford Molding, mainly because its tooling is cheaper. Nobody raised a strong objection to that direction.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Wexford Molding for the Granite enclosure.
- The main reason is that the tooling from Wexford Molding is cheaper.
