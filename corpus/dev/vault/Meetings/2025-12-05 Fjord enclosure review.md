Rosa, Diego and Ines met to compare the two tooling offers for the Fjord enclosure and settle on a direction for the vendor.

## Discussion

Ines put the two offers side by side: the [[Fjord enclosure - Cobalt Molding|offer from Cobalt Molding]] and the [[Fjord enclosure - Juno Plastics|offer from Juno Plastics]]. Both cover the tooling for the enclosure, both include parts from the tool for fit checks and drop testing, and both start work only once Larkspur gives the tooling go-ahead.

Rosa checked each offer against the enclosure section of the [[Fjord requirements]]: surviving the knocks of a busy gym, vents that keep dust out, mounting on block walls and battery access without taking the unit down. She walked the group through how each supplier responded to the drawings.

Most of the discussion was about price. Diego wants the tooling cost kept in check alongside the unit cost target, and Ines laid out how the two offers compare commercially. Lead time came up too, but the group spent much less time on it. On price, the team leaned toward Cobalt Molding.

Rosa reminded everyone that the enclosure still has to pass drop testing before tooling is released, as the requirements say.

## Decisions

- The team chose to move forward with Cobalt Molding for the Fjord enclosure.
