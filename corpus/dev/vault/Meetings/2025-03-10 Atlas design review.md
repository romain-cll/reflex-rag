Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Hazel Osei met for the design review of Atlas. Most of the session went to one question that is still open. Open questions are tracked on the [[Atlas]] project page.

## Discussion

The team started with a short walk through the current state of the design, covering the electronics, the enclosure and how the two fit together.

The rest of the time went to power. The team discussed switching to USB-C power for Atlas but made no decision. Zoe described what it would mean for the electronics, since the power input and the way the board manages power would both change. Rosa pointed out that it would also touch the enclosure, because the opening for the connector would move. Hector wondered how it would feel for people installing the device in the field. Hazel wanted to see the consequences for the product before taking a position.

The conversation went around in circles for quite a while. Each person could see the appeal, and each could see a cost, but nobody was ready to settle it in the room.

## Decisions

- No decision was made on USB-C power. The question stays open.

## Action items

- Hazel to send a recap and keep the question listed as open.
- Everyone to think about what switching would change in their own area before the next review.
