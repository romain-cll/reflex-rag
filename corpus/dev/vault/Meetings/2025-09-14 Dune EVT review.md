Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak, Hector Haddad and Claire Gallagher met to review the first engineering validation build of Dune, now that the enclosure tooling was ready.

## Discussion

The Dune EVT build produced 75 units. Zoe opened by describing how the boards came together in assembly and what the electrical bring-up looked like. Rosa looked at how the parts fit together inside the enclosure, and she confirmed that the enclosures were built as agreed at the sign-off recorded in [[2025-07-20 Dune tooling sign-off]].

Claire went through the inspection of the units from the quality side and described how the group should record what it sees on them. Hector asked how the findings from this build would be passed on to the next stage so that nothing gets lost between builds. The group agreed that this first build is mainly there to find problems early, while there is still room to change the design.

## Decisions

No change to the design was decided in the meeting. Findings from the units will be collected and discussed at the next review.

## Action items

- Each attendee to write down what they saw on the units and share it with the group.
- Send a recap of the review to everyone who attended.
- Schedule a follow-up review once the findings are collected.
