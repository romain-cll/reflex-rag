Ruth, Tessa, Rafael and Diego met for the Cirrus design review. Most of the meeting went to a single open question.

## Discussion

The question was whether Cirrus should have an e-ink display on the front of the unit. It came back several times and the group went around it at length.

Diego made the case for it. Teachers, coaches and facilities staff could see the air quality in the room at a glance, without opening a dashboard, and that could matter in a gym or an auditorium full of people.

Ruth raised power. An e-ink panel only draws power when it refreshes, but frequent refreshes would still eat into the battery life the requirements ask for, and she could not yet say by how much.

Tessa raised the enclosure. A display needs a window in the front face, which complicates the housing and adds a weak spot in a room where things get thrown around. It would also mean more work on the enclosure design at a point where the team wants it to settle.

Rafael raised the firmware side: driving the display, deciding what to show and how often to refresh it, and keeping that work from crowding out the rest of the firmware plan.

Cost came up as well, since any display adds to the unit cost against the target.

The group looked at it from every angle but could not agree. Some felt it was what would make the product stand out; others felt it put the battery and the enclosure at risk for a nice-to-have.

## Decisions

- The team discussed adding an e-ink display for Cirrus but made no decision. The question stays open.
