Rosa Novak, Carmen Iverson, Ines Fitzgerald and Naomi Boyle met to sign off on the Dune enclosure vendor now that the drop tests are complete.

## Discussion

Naomi Boyle walked the group through the drop tests on the enclosure samples and the validation steps that followed. Rosa added the mechanical view and went over what the tooling has to deliver for the enclosure to match the samples that were tested. Nobody raised an objection to closing the validation.

Ines confirmed that procurement has what it needs to release the tooling as soon as the go-ahead is recorded, and that the vendor contact is ready to start. Carmen asked that the outcome be written down clearly, because the enclosure vendor had been discussed earlier in the project and the team needs one reference from now on. This sign-off replaces the direction taken at the earlier enclosure review.

## Decisions

- Cobalt Molding received the tooling go-ahead for the Dune enclosure after the drop tests.
- [[Cobalt Molding]] is now the validated vendor for the Dune enclosure.
- This sign-off settles the enclosure section of [[Dune requirements]].
