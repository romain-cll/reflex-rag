Hazel Osei and Claire Gallagher met to kick off the Atlas pilot with the customer. The goal was to agree on the scope of the pilot and the number of units involved.

## Discussion

The customer is [[Westgate Realty Partners]]. Westgate Realty Partners is piloting Atlas with 80 units. Hazel described the pilot as a way to get real feedback from the customer before the launch.

Claire explained what quality will be watching during the pilot: how the units behave once installed, and how any issue gets reported and traced back to a build. Both agreed that anything found in the field should be written down in one place so that nothing gets lost between the customer and the team.

For project context, the page of [[Atlas]] remains the place to look for the overall status of the work.

## Decisions

- The pilot goes ahead at the customer with the units agreed above.
- Issues found during the pilot are collected in one place and shared with the team.
