Emeka Nordin met with Clara Zielinski and Fatima Foley for the monthly operations review. After the usual standing items, the group spent its time on one proposal.

## Discussion

The meeting began with the routine items: supplier follow-ups, the state of shipments and a check on work that has been waiting on someone. Nothing needed escalation, and each owner keeps the details of their own item.

The main topic was a proposal about tooling. The leadership team discussed moving the sales team to a new CRM. Supporters pointed to a cleaner view of the pipeline and easier reporting. The concerns were the effort of migrating existing records, the time the sales team would spend learning a new tool, and the risk of disrupting customer follow-up while the switch happens. Clara and Fatima both asked how the new CRM would connect to the tools already in use, and Emeka agreed that this question has no answer yet.

The conversation was open and friendly, and no one asked to settle it in the room.

## Decisions

The leadership team made no decision on the CRM. The proposal stays open.
