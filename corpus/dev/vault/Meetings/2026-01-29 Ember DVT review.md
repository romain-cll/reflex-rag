Naomi Boyle led the design validation review of Ember with Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor and Hazel Osei, focused on the main issue found on the DVT units.

## Discussion

Naomi presented the main finding of this round: the Ember DVT units showed a CO2 reading drift above 35 degrees Celsius. Once the units got warmer than that, the CO2 values wandered away from where they should be. She called it the issue that matters most from DVT and asked that it be treated as such.

Ruth wants to separate the possible causes before anyone proposes a fix: the sensor itself, the way its readings are corrected, or heat building up inside the enclosure. Tessa will look at the enclosure side and how heat moves around the sensing elements. Hazel asked what the reading drift would mean for customers; the honest answer for now is that the team does not know yet.

DVT units also went to the pilot agreed at the [[2026-01-19 Ember pilot kickoff with Larchmont College]], so the team needs to know whether any of the pilot buildings get warm enough for the drift to show. Hazel will check conditions on site with the facilities team at Larchmont College.

There is no root cause yet and no decision on a fix. The issue stays open until the investigation comes back.

## Action items

- Ruth: lead the investigation into the reading drift, starting with the sensor and how its readings are corrected.
- Tessa: review the heat path inside the enclosure.
- Hazel: check conditions at the pilot site with the facilities team.
- Naomi: track the issue to closure and send a recap to the attendees.
