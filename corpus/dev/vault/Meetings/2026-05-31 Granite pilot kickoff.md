Carmen Iverson and Claire Gallagher met to kick off the Granite pilot with the customer and to agree on its scope and the number of units.

## Discussion

[[Redwood Hills Realty Partners]] is the customer for the pilot, and it will try Granite in its own buildings. Carmen walked through what the pilot is meant to show and how the team will work with the customer during it. Claire explained how quality issues reported during the pilot will be collected and followed up.

## Decisions

- The pilot covers units installed in the customer's buildings, with feedback gathered from the customer during use.
- The full project status is kept on the project page, [[Granite]].
