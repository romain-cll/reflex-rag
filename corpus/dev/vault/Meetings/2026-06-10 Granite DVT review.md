Claire Gallagher, Ruth Holloway, Tessa Okafor and Carmen Iverson met for the design validation review of Granite, with a focus on the main issue found on the DVT units.

## Discussion

The Granite DVT units showed Wi-Fi dropouts near metal ceiling grids. Claire described where and how the dropouts appeared during the validation work. Ruth looked at the issue from the radio and electrical side, and Tessa from the mechanical side, including how the sensor sits relative to the surrounding metal.

The group talked about whether the cause is in the electrical design, in the placement of the sensor or in a mix of both, and did not settle on an answer in the meeting. The DVT units also went to the pilot described in [[2026-05-31 Granite pilot kickoff]], so the finding is relevant to the customer experience as well. Carmen asked that the team keep her informed as the cause becomes clearer.

## Action items

- Claire will send a recap of the review with the test records.
- Ruth and Tessa will compare notes on the dropouts.
- The team will schedule a follow-up meeting to look into the cause.
