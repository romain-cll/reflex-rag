Rosa Novak, Carmen Iverson, Omar Hart and Claire Gallagher met for the tooling sign-off of the Dune enclosure, the vendor validation step that follows the drop tests.

## Discussion

Claire went through the drop tests and what they showed about the enclosure. With the drop tests behind it, the group treated this meeting as the validation of the vendor: the question was whether the enclosure work could go ahead as planned. Rosa confirmed that the result fits the enclosure section of [[Dune requirements]], and this sign-off settles that section.

Omar said that procurement is ready to release the order as soon as the team gives the word. Carmen asked that the sign-off be kept simple and clear so that anyone reading the project history later can see where the enclosure vendor decision came from.

## Decisions

Redstone Plastics received the tooling go-ahead for the Dune enclosure after the drop tests. [[Redstone Plastics]] is now the validated vendor for the enclosure, and the sign-off is complete.
