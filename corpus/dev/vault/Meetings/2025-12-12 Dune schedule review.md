Carmen Iverson, Naomi Boyle and Emeka Nordin met to review the Dune schedule after learning that a certification test has to be run again.

## Discussion

Naomi explained the situation. A certification test for Dune has to be repeated, and the retest will run at [[Redstone Compliance Labs]], the lab that handles the certification work. The product cannot ship until the retest is done and the report is in hand, so the launch has to wait for it.

Carmen walked through the impact on the plan. Once the retest is booked and the time for the report is added, the original launch date no longer holds. She proposed a new date with some margin built in, rather than a tight date that might slip a second time.

Emeka asked whether anything else on the plan depends on the old date and whether the team had looked at ways to recover time. Carmen said the retest is the critical path and that the rest of the work can move with it. Emeka preferred a date the team can actually meet over an optimistic one, and agreed with the proposal.

## Decisions

- Dune will now launch on August 2, 2026.
- The Dune launch slipped because of a certification retest at Redstone Compliance Labs.
