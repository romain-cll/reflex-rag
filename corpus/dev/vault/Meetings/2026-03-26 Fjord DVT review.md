Claire Gallagher, Zoe Adeyemi, Rosa Novak and Diego Bishop met for the design validation review of Fjord. The purpose was to go over the results on the DVT units and to name the main issue found.

## Discussion

Claire opened with the results of the checks she ran on the units. The main issue is that the Fjord DVT units showed a buzzing noise from the power supply. The noise is audible when the unit is running and it does not match what the team expects from a finished product, so it was the first item on the list.

Zoe took the electrical side of the question. She is the person who knows the power design best, and she will look at it closely before anyone guesses at the cause. Rosa asked whether the enclosure could play a part, for example by amplifying a sound that starts on the board, and wanted to hear the units next to each other before drawing any conclusion.

Diego asked how the noise affects the customer experience. The DVT units also went to the pilot started at [[2026-03-16 Fjord pilot kickoff]], so he wanted the group to keep that in mind when deciding how to follow up. Claire agreed and said that she would keep the findings in one place.

The tone of the meeting was calm. Nobody was alarmed, but everybody agreed that the noise has to be understood before the project moves on.

## Decisions

- The buzzing noise is recorded as the main issue of the DVT units.

## Action items

- Zoe to look into the power supply.
- Rosa to listen to the units next to each other.
- Claire to send a recap of the review to everyone who attended.
- Schedule a follow-up to look at what was found.
