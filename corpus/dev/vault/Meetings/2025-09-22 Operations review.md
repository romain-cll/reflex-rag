Emeka Nordin met with Clara Zielinski and Fatima Foley for the monthly operations review.

## Discussion

The standing items went quickly. Each owner gave a short status, and nothing on the list needed to be escalated.

The main topic was a proposal to move the sales team to a new CRM. Emeka brought it to the leadership team as an open question rather than a plan.

Clara Zielinski asked what problem a new CRM would solve that the current tools do not, and how much effort the move would take. Fatima Foley pointed to the cost of any switch: moving the existing records over, training the sales team, and the risk of a slow stretch while people get used to a new tool.

Emeka noted that Sales would need to be part of the conversation before anything is settled, since the change would land on them first. The discussion went around a few times without anyone asking for a call either way, and the leadership team made no decision on the CRM.

## Decisions

- None. Moving the sales team to a new CRM was discussed, and the question remains open.
