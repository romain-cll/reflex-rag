This document sets out the requirements for Granite. It builds on what the team agreed at [[2025-10-25 Granite kickoff]] and is owned by Carmen Iverson. It covers power, cost, the enclosure and the battery pack.

## Overview

Engineering uses this document as the reference when it makes design choices, and procurement uses it when it asks suppliers for offers. Each section below states what the design has to meet and what supplier work is needed to meet it. When a section depends on a supplier, the written offer of that supplier is read against the section before anything is ordered.

## Power

The Granite requirements call for a battery life of 53 months. Power draw is therefore a central design constraint. Every electrical choice, from the sensing elements to the radio, is judged against that target, and the electrical engineers own the power budget. Any change that puts the target at risk is raised with the product manager as soon as it is known, so that the trade-off is made on purpose and not by accident.

## Cost

The target unit cost for Granite is $44. The target is the reference for every supplier offer and every design trade-off. A choice that makes a unit more expensive has to be justified by a gain against another requirement, and the product manager reviews those choices before they are accepted.

## Enclosure

The enclosure has to protect the electronics, keep the sensing openings clear and withstand normal handling and installation. Because the enclosure is a molded part, it needs tooling built by a supplier. Procurement asks molding suppliers for offers on that tooling, and each offer is checked against this section for scope, price and delivery time. The mechanical engineers review every offer for fit with the design.

## Battery pack

The battery pack must deliver the battery life set out in the power section. The pack is a purchased part, so procurement asks battery suppliers for offers and compares them against the battery life and the unit cost target. The mechanical and electrical engineers confirm that the pack fits the enclosure and the power budget before it is accepted.

## Decisions

The enclosure supplier decision is recorded in the tooling sign-off, [[2026-01-08 Granite tooling sign-off]]. Anyone with a question about the enclosure supplier should start with that sign-off.
