## Overview

Working copy of the Cirrus requirements, for the Larkspur Vista Gen 2. I started it right after [[2025-03-21 Cirrus kickoff]] so the hardware side could mark up the power and enclosure sections while the requirements were still taking shape.

The product goes into large shared rooms at schools. It is installed and looked after by school facilities staff, not by specialists: simple to mount, simple to maintain, hard to break.

## Power

- The unit runs on batteries, so it can be mounted wherever the readings are needed, not only where there is an outlet.
- The Cirrus requirements call for a battery life of 30 months under the normal reporting schedule.
- The battery must be replaceable on site by facilities staff, without sending the unit back.
- The unit must warn the facilities team about a low battery well before it stops reporting.
- Any feature that adds to the power draw must be weighed against the battery life requirement.

## Sensor module

- The sensor module comes from an outside supplier and must fit within the cost target.
- Readings must stay stable in rooms that go from empty to crowded and back several times a day.
- Procurement collects supplier offers for the module before the design is frozen.

## Cost

- Target unit cost: still to be filled in from the product side.
- Enclosure tooling is a one-time cost and is tracked separately from the unit cost.
- Design changes that put the cost at risk must be raised with the product owner early.

## Enclosure

- The enclosure must survive life in a gym: knocks from balls and equipment, and drops during installation.
- It must pass drop testing before tooling is released.
- It mounts on a wall or on a ceiling structure with hardware that facilities staff can handle on their own.
- The battery compartment must stay closed in normal use and open easily for a battery swap.
- Wall thickness and rib layout to be reviewed with the enclosure suppliers once their feedback comes in.
- Procurement will compare tooling offers from more than one enclosure supplier.
