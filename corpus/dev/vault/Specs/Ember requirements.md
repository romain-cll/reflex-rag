This document sets out the requirements of Ember. It builds on what the team agreed at [[2025-07-06 Ember kickoff]] and is owned by Hazel Osei as product owner. Each section below states what the product has to meet, and the offers we receive from suppliers are checked against it.

## Overview

Ember is a Larkspur sensor project, and Larkspur outsources manufacturing, so most of the requirements below end up as terms in a request to a supplier. They cover four areas: power, cost, the enclosure and the circuit board assembly. This document is approved and is the reference the team uses whenever a question about scope comes up. A change to any requirement goes through the product owner and is recorded here.

## Power

The Ember requirements call for a battery life of 48 months. The power budget of the electronics has to be designed around this requirement, from the sensing elements to the connectivity. Any design choice that would shorten battery life must be raised with the product owner before it is adopted, even if it looks small on paper.

## Cost

The target unit cost for Ember is $67. This target is the yardstick for every supplier offer received for the project. An offer that fits the technical needs but pushes the unit cost beyond the target goes back to the product owner for a decision, and procurement keeps the running comparison up to date.

## Enclosure

The enclosure protects the electronics and has to hold up to the handling a sensor sees in everyday use. Mechanical engineering leads this section. Tooling for the enclosure is requested from plastics vendors, and procurement collects their offers so that price and delivery time can be set side by side against the cost target above.

## Circuit board assembly

The circuit board carries the sensing and power electronics. Electrical engineering owns the board design, and a contract manufacturer assembles the boards, with pricing given per board. Procurement collects the offers for the assembly and compares them with the cost target, in the same way as for the enclosure.

## Decisions

The enclosure supplier decision is recorded in [[2025-09-19 Ember tooling sign-off]]. The enclosure section above is settled by that sign-off, so the decision is not repeated here and the sign-off stays the single place to look.
