This document sets the requirements for Atlas. It was written right after [[2025-01-09 Atlas kickoff]] and is owned by Hazel Osei. Each section below states what the design must meet, and supplier offers are measured against it.

## Overview

Atlas is a connected sensor, and the requirements below cover the hardware as a whole: power, cost, the enclosure and the circuit board assembly. When a requirement touches more than one discipline, the product owner has the final say. This version is approved and serves as the reference for the design work.

## Power

The Atlas requirements call for a battery life of 30 months. The choice of cells and the sleep behavior of the electronics have to be judged against that figure. Any change that could affect battery life should go back to the product owner before it is built into the design.

## Cost

Atlas has a target unit cost for the finished unit. It has to cover the enclosure, the assembled circuit board and everything else that ships in the box. Procurement checks each offer against the target and raises any offer that puts it at risk.

## Enclosure

The enclosure protects the electronics and has to hold the board and the battery securely. It is made of tooled plastic parts from an outside molder, so this section asks each supplier for the price of the tooling and for how long it takes to deliver it. Hardware engineering reviews every offer against the mechanical needs described here.

## Circuit board assembly

An outside contract manufacturer assembles the circuit board. This section asks for a price per board so that the assembly cost can be compared with the unit cost target. Hardware engineering looks at each offer for fit with the board design before anything is agreed.
