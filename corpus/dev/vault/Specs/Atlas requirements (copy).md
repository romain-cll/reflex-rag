*Working copy of the Atlas requirements, used for mechanical edits.*

## Overview

This document sets the requirements for Atlas, the sensor for open-plan offices. It was written right after the [[2025-01-25 Atlas kickoff]] to capture what the team agreed while it was fresh, and to give engineering and procurement targets to work against. Each section states what the product must do. Open points are flagged in the section where they come up.

The audience is the Atlas core team: product, hardware engineering, firmware and procurement.

## Power

- Atlas runs on a battery so it can be placed anywhere on an office floor without depending on outlets.
- The Atlas requirements call for a battery life of 18 months in normal office use.
- Firmware must keep the radio and the sensors in low-power modes between readings so that the battery target holds.
- Replacing the battery must be simple enough for a building facilities team to do on site, without special tools.

## Cost

- The unit cost has to stay low enough for a product positioned as the low-cost option for open-plan offices.
- Every design choice in the sections below should be checked against the cost target. Anything that puts it at risk goes back to the product owner.
- Tooling is a one-time cost and is tracked apart from the unit cost.

## Enclosure

- The enclosure must look discreet in an open-plan office and be easy to mount on a wall or a column.
- Vents must let room air reach the sensors without letting dust build up inside.
- The enclosure must protect the electronics during shipping and installation, and it has to pass drop testing before tooling is released.
- The battery must be reachable without taking the unit off its mount.
- Wall thickness, rib layout and the vent pattern are still being worked out and will be added here.
- Procurement collects tooling offers from enclosure suppliers against this section.

## Circuit board assembly

- The circuit board is assembled by an outside supplier.
- Offers should be priced per board and cover assembly and basic testing of each board.
- The supplier must be able to scale from engineering builds to production volumes without a change of process.
